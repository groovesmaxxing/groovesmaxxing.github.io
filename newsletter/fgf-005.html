miami just went and did it

remember when i said we're going to III points? the satellite program dropped this week and october 16-17 quietly became october 13-20. for the first time satellite runs a full week: fifteen-plus venues, one hundred-plus artists, 250-plus hours of music across seven miami neighborhoods, all orbiting the main festival at mana wynwood.

maccabi house takes over hialeah park casino. sunday october 18, 3 to 11pm, in the garden. adam ten x mita gami, rafael, yamagucci, miluhska. the label this newsletter won't shut up about, running its own room at a casino. we'll be there.

the 33-hour closing marathon at club space. doors sunday 11pm, closing time officially "unknown," runs into tuesday. set times read like our playlists: honey dijon at 12:30am, adam ten b2b mita gami at 2am, marco carola at 5am, brunello b2b rony seikaly at 11am, seth troxler b2b maher daniel at 8pm, max styler b2b OMRI. at 11pm. sunrise on the space terrace with that lineup is a bucket-list item.

the main festival itself. max styler b2b vintage culture, marco carola b2b franky rizardo, brunello b2b rafael, PAWSA, cloonee, KETTAMA, and odd mob on the bill, with charlotte de witte, four tet and underworld up top.

tickets. satellite is presale-first: hold a festival ticket by 11:59pm et any day and your code lands at 1:11pm the next day. general on-sale opened monday august 24 at 1:11pm et.
