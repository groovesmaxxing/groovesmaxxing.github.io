the artists wrote back 🤝

this is the part we are most proud of. five rows this week were confirmed by the artist who made the record, in their own words, not by a guess of ours.

Davidi told us the track at 9:55 in adam ten's barcelona set is his own, "ride", and that our timestamp was off. we moved it. he came back again on rafael's set: the michael jackson edit at 27:56 is his and Michael (ME)'s, and it is still unreleased.

GROSSOMODDO confirmed "brrr" in our dms. still unreleased, out in october.

Jackie answered two rows in one message, "let me house u" and the mama remix, both still unreleased.

other asks are still sitting unanswered, and those rows stay exactly as they are until we hear back. we would rather leave a row open than put a name on it that nobody confirmed.

if we got something wrong, tell us. we will fix it the same day.
