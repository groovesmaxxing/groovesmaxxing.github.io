the groove exchange 📈

we built a stock exchange for house music. no money, nothing to buy, nothing to sell. just a board where every record we track gets a price, and the price moves every day with how hard the dance floor is backing it.

how it works. every track gets one closing price a day, from 5 to 250 points. dj support does most of the lifting (where a record ranks for it, and whether that's growing this week), then its beatport chart tier, then spotify editorial adds. everything is ranked against the rest of the board, so a record only climbs by outrunning the others. the full recipe is written out at the bottom of the page.

what's next. this is version one and we're not done with it. artists and labels are next on the list, and the board gets bigger as we track more records. if there's something you'd want it to show, hit reply and tell us.
