the grammys 🏆

the 2027 grammy nominations are out. the dance/electronic categories went to Madonna, Robyn, Slayyyter, Tame Impala, Skrillex, Fred again.., Tinashe and underscores, and there's real talent on that list. John Summit, one of the biggest touring DJs in the country, came up empty for his album CTRL ESCAPE and said what a lot of the scene was thinking.

we think he's right about one thing: dance music has outgrown two categories. back in 2023 the academy added best dance pop recording so pop records would get their own lane, and that was a good call. the next step is a lane for the records built for the club: house, tech house, techno, afro house, the music DJs actually play out. this year not one of them made the list.

so we're with Summit on the fix: more categories for true dance music, judged by people who know the dance floor. nobody has to lose a spot for that to happen. there's room for all of it. always all grooves, all vibes. 🏆

nominees via grammy.com and stereogum.

meanwhile in vice city 🎮

and then GTA VI dropped thursday and everyone is talking about it. 13 years of waiting, people calling in with the "GTA flu," and the six album singles pulled 4.1 million streams in their first day back in september.

the part we can't stop playing: rockstar gave house and techno a whole station. circoloco records radio, hosted by Seth Troxler and circoloco cofounder antonio carbonaro, and it's stacked.

Michael Bibi & KETTAMA, MYSTERY OF RAW (ft. wu-tang clan)

built on "da mystery of chessboxin'," a record KETTAMA first sent Bibi back in 2018, cleared by wu-tang themselves, out on circoloco records since august.

via cultr

black lounge, bickering (Max Dean remix)

a staple of Max Dean's festival sets that's only ever surfaced in mixes. unreleased, and now it's on the radio in vice city.

via cultr

more unreleased on the station

CHRIS STASSY "we on this ride", Chloé Caillet & Myd "i feel love" and DJ Tennis "never look back". plus Michael Bibi "rhythm of the night", PAWSA & fast eddie "body rock FM" and Seth Troxler "bootlicker", with Skream, Prospa, Mind Against and ANOTR in the rotation.

via dj mag and we rave you

a lot of the station is music you can't buy yet. expect those IDs in every set this winter. we'll be hunting them.

and it's not just a game moment. house and techno have been on the way up all year: the electronic music business hit $15.1 billion in 2025, up 7% (IMS 2026). tech house is still beatport's top genre for a fourth year with house right behind it, and afro house went from 10th to 2nd on splice in two years. now the most hyped game in years gave the club its own station. that chart only goes one way. 📈

and if the game got you on PAWSA, his WORLDWIDE lands next friday. it's in dropping soon below.
