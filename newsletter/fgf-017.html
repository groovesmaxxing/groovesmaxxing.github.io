the grammys 🏆

0

house or techno records

in either dance/electronic list

3

pop acts in both lanes

Madonna, Slayyyter, Tinashe

the 2027 grammy nominations are out. the dance/electronic categories went to Madonna, Robyn, Slayyyter, Tame Impala, Skrillex, Fred again.., Tinashe and underscores, and there's real talent on that list. John Summit, one of the biggest touring DJs in the country, came up empty for his album CTRL ESCAPE and said what a lot of the scene was thinking.

we think he's right about one thing: dance music has outgrown two categories. back in 2023 the academy added best dance pop recording so pop records would get their own lane, and that was a good call. the next step is a lane for the records built for the club: house, tech house, techno, afro house, the music DJs actually play out.

more categories for true dance music, judged by people who know the dance floor. there's room for all of it. always all grooves, all vibes. 🏆

nominees via grammy.com and stereogum.
