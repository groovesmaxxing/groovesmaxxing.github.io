the talk of the floor 🗣️

on sept 27 John Summit posted: "idc what genre u listen to the only thing that is killing the dance floor is the constant yapping."

a day later Max Styler backed him up with the cleanest rule we've seen: "do the drugs, do the alcohol, do the dancing, let your freak flag fly but don't open your trap."

some clubs got there first. Nowadays in new york asks people to keep floor convos short and take the long ones somewhere else.

our take: the dance floor is not the line for the bathroom. need to catch up, the patio exists. need a track ID, that's literally our job, just ask us.

so are we a no-yap household now? hit reply and tell us. the best answer goes in next week's issue. (the full story)
