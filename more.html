    about

    we listen to everything. you get the good stuff. house, techno and indie dance, curated like a friend texting you bangers. no label pays for a spot, no promo pool, no pay-to-play. if it's here, it goes.

    the scan listens for about 15 seconds and looks for the track in our own library of sets first, then in the catalogs. nothing is ever marked confirmed, and every result has a button to tell us we got it wrong.
