      the gdb pipeline

      AYYBO, anaheim's own, runs GDB out of los angeles, and the bench runs deep: Gabss, JATO, N2N, IVRISH, POLOVICH, DEDRO, with Millero on remix duty. when your most-played artist is also signing your next favorite, the algorithm didn't find them. you did. label > algorithm.

      the nu moda pipeline

      Max Styler launched Nu Moda in october 2025 and made it home for his own records. the bench: kryptogram, Greggio, Knorst, Jonathan, DvirNuns, Viot, WELKER. the co-signs live here too: Vintage Culture and Notre Dame on You & Me, Solomun on One More.

      the solid grooves crew

      Michael Bibi and PAWSA ran Solid Grooves as a london party before it was ever a label, and the label came in 2015. bibi put the party on, PAWSA minded the music. the bench since: Dennis Cruz, ANOTR, Ben Sterling, Toman, Kolter, Discip, Josh Butler, plus AYYBO on 4 Ya Mind. summers belong to DC-10, the casino nights in ibiza.

      why this exists

      groovesmaxxing started the way every good obsession does: one person sending way too many songs to the group chat. the playlists got long. the follows got longer. eventually "you have to hear this" needed a bigger room than a text thread. so here we are.

      the rules are simple. fun first. this page exists because sharing music you love is one of life's genuinely great feelings, and for no other reason. zero budget. everything here runs on free tools and stubbornness. no pay-to-play. no ads, no sponsors, nobody sliding into the inbox to buy a slot. if a track is here, it's because it slaps. lowercase forever. corporate never.

      what that adds up to: new releases filtered down every friday so you don't have to dig, live sets that actually earn their runtime, artist deep-dives on the people who define the sound, and the contraband: edits, bootlegs and unreleased heat that never touches a streaming service. consider this your friend with permanent aux privileges.
