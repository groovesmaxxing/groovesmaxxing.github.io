how we do it

a lot of these sets were tagged before we got to them: by the artist, by the channel that posted it, or by the heads in the comments. we find those lists, line them up against the set and say whose work it is.

the rest we tag ourselves, with fingerprints and the only two ears over here. those timestamps are measured to five seconds.

labels are checked against a catalogue, or left off.

a track nobody could name says ID instead of a guess. a (?) means one source so far.

audio, crowd, artist and ear after a track say how we know the name: a fingerprint, a listener, the artist, or our own ears.

got one wrong or know an ID? tell us and we'll fix it asap 🤝
