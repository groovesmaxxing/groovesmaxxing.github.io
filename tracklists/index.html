how we do it

we listen to as many sets as we can, but there are only two ears over here. so every timestamp is measured to five seconds.

labels are checked against a catalogue, or left off.

a track we couldn't name says ID instead of a guess. a (?) means one source so far.

audio, crowd, artist and ear after a track say how we know the name: a fingerprint, a listener, the artist, or our own ears.

got one wrong or know an ID? tell us and we'll fix it asap 🤝
