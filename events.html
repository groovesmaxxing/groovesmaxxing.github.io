the field guide

denver venues

seventeen rooms where the grooves actually happen, from an 1865 cathedral to the big Red Rocks. we checked every single one, and every single one is open. the clubs first, then the big rooms.

the clubs · dance floors first

The Church

Capitol Hill · 1160 Lincoln St · cap ~1,550 · ages vary by night

the longest-running dance club in the city, open since 1996 inside an 1865 cathedral. Global Fridays every week, big house and melodic bookings on saturdays.

Club Vinyl

Broadway · 1082 N Broadway · cap ~2,470 · 21+

four floors and a rooftop on Broadway with Funktion-One sound. the basement is where the proper house and techno lives.

site ↗instagram ↗ra ↗

Bar Standard

Broadway · 1037 N Broadway · cap ~750 · 21+

the house and techno room of the Broadway strip, inside an art moderne furrier building. TheHundred Presents runs the underground through here.

site ↗instagram ↗ra ↗

Milk

Broadway · alley behind 1037 N Broadway · cap ~970 · 21+

the basement under Bar Standard, entrance in the alley. new wave, darkwave and indie dance inside a Clockwork Orange fever dream.

site ↗instagram ↗ra ↗

The Black Box

Capitol Hill · 314 E 13th Ave · cap ~350 · 18+

Sub.mission's room, dubstep, 140 and drum and bass on the custom Basscouch. more bass than house, still essential. Electronic Tuesdays every week.

site ↗instagram ↗ra ↗

The Beacon

RiNo · 2854 Larimer St

an art bar built on local djs instead of headliners. minimal, tech house and raw grooves most nights, and surprise sets happen.
