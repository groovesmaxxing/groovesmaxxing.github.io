VOLAC

🚧 bumpy moscow, russia bass house 🎧 in rotation · 4 🚧 ▶️ sets on file

VOLAC is a moscow duo built around heavy house with rolling, chest deep bass. dancing astronaut has called them a russian pair since at least 2016. their tracks favor thick low end and chopped vocal hooks, with a groove that leans into tech house.

early releases landed on night bass, the g-house label run by AC slater, and on CUFF. in 2019 they teamed with dr. fresch for Filthy, a house cut with dread MC on the mic, released on tchami's confession.

they opened 2020 with Baby Boy on musical freedom, tiësto's label. between 2023 and 2025 they returned repeatedly to repopulate mars.

since september 2025, most of their releases have come through myth of NYX, including Like Ice, out 22 may 2026.

no upcoming shows are listed on their own site or on the platforms checked, as of 3 august 2026.
