Mario Eighta

🚧 bumpy mexico melodic techno + indie dance

Mario Eighta makes melodic techno and indie dance out of mexico, the country on the soundcloud profile. the soundcloud bio tags a long list of labels, from errorr, impressum and eleatics to be free, astral, automatik and surrrealism, and the instagram bio leads with siona, errorr, impressum and eleatics, then a line that reads label: @earlyreflections_.

the spotify catalogue starts with Amethyst with fernando de alba in march 2020, and a lot of the early run went out with fenix j, from Gehena in 2021 to the Skyfall EP in 2023. 2024 brought System Failure and BRAVE HEART, then Arggon and U-70P17 with MANAT. in 2025 came Star Command and Hurts on impressum, Madting on automatik, Epitomme with ignorancia t on surrrealism and a ReVision of emiliano demarco's Android on astral records.

2026 is the busiest year on the catalogue. the SET YOU FREE EP landed on errorr in january, Afraid Of The Dark on automatik in february and Drowning with emiliano demarco on NOW in april, with a run on early reflections from Mario Eighta & Friends in may to Bodywork and Bleu in the summer. the BOOM EP with emiliano demarco came out on errorr on 21 august, a re-tribe of alex galvan and andec james' Silensis with leonardo (MX) on satelite records in september, and 17 with miss monique and henri bergmann on siona records on 16 october. BOOM and the VUH remix of silensis both sit in our bumpy playlist.
