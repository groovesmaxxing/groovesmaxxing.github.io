Max Dean

🚧 bumpy london house 👀 one to watch · 1 🚧 🏷️ label boss ▶️ sets on file

Max Dean is a London house producer who runs the DX3N label and works constantly with his cousin Luke Dean.

Recent output crosses into the majors: Not A Problem with Nafe Smallz and Luke Dean on Interscope, Can't Decide on FFRR, and a Jamie Jones remix of Gets Like That. He also remixed Timbaland and Mason Collective.

Bumpy, vocal-driven house built for big rooms.
