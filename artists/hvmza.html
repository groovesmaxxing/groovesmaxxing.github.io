HVMZA

belgium afro + latin + organic house

HVMZA is a belgian producer and dj with turkish and arabic roots, in the soundcloud bio's words, with music between afro, latin and organic house that pulls in middle eastern, latin american folk and african rhythms. the bio adds a self-taught start: hip hop in a bedroom with cousins and friends, then youtube tutorials, then house.

the debut single was Na Bosana with gaz mawete on qukka burra in 2019, and qukka burra carried most of what came next: Sokho, Selvi, Rosa, La Critica, Coco, Mama, Quimey, Mogo (the top track on last.fm), Altura, Deedee, Turiya and Narcos. Dame with el mukuka went out on tonspiel, Na Mela on cafe de anatolia and Ancora Qui on hysterical.

since 2025: Zurna II and Estarabim with burak cilt and erkin koray on WE'RE HERE, DeeDee II on deep root tribe, Dibelel with divolly & markward on make the girls dance, a run of turkish-titled self-releases, and XO with juany bravo on WTR by MDLBEAST on 8 october 2026.
