Clüb De Combat

🚧 bumpy tel aviv, israel acid, disco and industrial house

clüb de combat are a duo out of tel aviv who came together in 2020. Amit and Aviv met through friends at a party, and both had been making music from around the age of fourteen, aviv on drums and amit on guitar, before either of them touched a laptop. they describe the sound themselves as a balance of acid, disco and nineties industrial house.

their run on diynamic, the label founded by Solomun, opened with the Time EP alongside rex the dog on 29 september 2023 and carried on with the Exciter EP with matt guy on 17 may 2024. beatport's top tracks list for them is led by their remix of atfc and lisa millett's Bad Habit, out on defected on 23 may 2025, with On Repeat right behind it, a record cut with Max Styler for John Summit's experts only on 13 december 2024.

asked about algorithmic discovery, they were careful about it.

They're useful, but they can blur the line between what you genuinely love and what just feels relevant.

clüb de combat to magnetic magazine, 6 may 2026

FIGHTCLÜB is their own imprint. magnetic magazine reported on 6 may 2026 that they launched it with Jack The House, a track with rudy with a hoodie that had been running in their sets for close to a year before it came out on 3 april 2026, and beatport also carries Wai Wai on the label from 7 february 2025. the Connection EP is due on it on 7 august 2026. away from their own label, All The Single Ladies landed on defected on 26 june 2026, which defected called their second release for the house.

they told beatportal that the night that sticks hardest was playing alongside Solomun in spain in heavy rain, equipment problems and all.

shotgun logs their 2025 festival run, odizia in aix-en-provence over 7 to 9 march and sandwaves in les anses-d'arlet on 17 may, plus a date at night we met on 13 march 2026, and their soundcloud carries a set recorded for house calls in chicago and posted on 8 april 2026. booking runs through CAA for the americas. no upcoming bookings are published on shotgun as of 2 august 2026, and they have no resident advisor artist page at all.
