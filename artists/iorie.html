Iorie

germany organic house + downtempo

Iorie is a producer, live act and dj from germany, in the soundcloud bio's words: singing and playing instruments from a young age, producing from 15, and music across all tempos with the sounds of travels and personal, philosophical lyrics in it. the same bio says the fun house side goes out as anna almani.

serafin audio imprint carries most of the catalog, from the album Bulanık Aşk in 2017 to Golden Spree, Italian Devine, the Guli Nateli EP with tamada, Matter Of Fact, Follow The Sun, Nighttime Tales with sydka, Nostalgia, Two Red Souls with bawab, Blacktop, the Daytime Diaries EP, Harmonics and 2015. the deezer list goes back to the And The Gang EP on ritter butzke studio in 2015.

elsewhere: Silence with gorje hewek and alexey union on watergate, Khululeka and Beijo, credited with anna almani, on love letter and stil vor talent, My Trip on katahaifisch, One Little Glitch on easy tiger, Fever Dream on poker flat in july 2026, Sequences with township rebellion and solee on hilomatik, and Hypnotherapy with franca on kiosk ID, a top four pick in fresh grooves friday 011.
