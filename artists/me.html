&ME

🧃 vibes berlin house 🎧 in rotation · 4 🧃 🌴 ▶️ sets on file

the third keinemusik founder on this page, and the one who leans furthest toward the song. his own bio describes the aim as linking innovative production with reception people actually want, and his catalogue bears that out: where Adam Port has the crossover hit and Rampa has the hardware company, &ME has the vocals.

the two records that carry his name furthest are both called the rapture. The Rapture Pt.II came out on keinemusik in november 2018. The Rapture Pt.III, made with Black Coffee and released in june 2023, is black coffee's most played track anywhere and one of the collective's biggest. earlier there was After Dark in 2014 and Garden in 2019.

he and rampa co produced two tracks on drake's Honestly, Nevermind in 2022. the stage name itself, per his bio, is meant as a small act of modesty.

the collective's own line for what all three of them are doing has not changed in fifteen years: refining house and techno off the beaten path. in april 2024 they took that to the pyramids at giza for a night, which is not a sentence you get to write about many labels.

his summer is european. çeşme on 4 august, twiga on the ninth, north coast in egypt on the fourteenth billed against rampa, circoloco at dc 10 on the seventeenth with him, then amstelveen, madrid, brussels, manchester and são paulo through to october.
