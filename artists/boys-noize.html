Boys Noize

berlin electro techno

Boys Noize is alex ridha, a berlin dj and producer. his own soundcloud bio has him making his dj debut at 15 and starting boysnoize records in 2005 to keep full artistic freedom over his own music. the same bio puts the label past 150 releases, with a sub-label, bnr trax.

the bio also lists the side projects, dog blood with skrillex, octave minds with chilly gonzales and handbraekes with mr. oizo, plus remixes for depeche mode, daft punk, david lynch, feist and nine inch nails. that last one came back around in april 2026 as Nine Inch Noize, an album of boys noize versions of nine inch nails songs, out on the null corporation through interscope.

the rest of 2026 has been just as busy. Get Away with kungs in march, I Bring My Gun To The Function with brutalismus 3000 in april, ITVLO on running back in june, then a run on ONES AND ZEROS: Shut It Down with taichu and taube, Baile DF, 1+1+1 with zep, Nächstenliebe with brutalismus 3000 and the album 7LLVSION on 6 november. with Tiga he made HOT WIFE, out on turbo in january and on tiga's album HOTLIFE in april, and the remixes by Ben Sterling and Fcukers followed later in the summer. a year before that, in may 2025, he was on Crazy For It with Rampa, Adam Port, &ME, vinson and Keinemusik.

He’s the techno punk, the unboxable nomad...
