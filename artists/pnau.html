PNAU

australia electronic

PNAU is an Australian electronic act, releasing since 2004, on records with Elton John, MEDUZA, Kungs and Rudimental.

PNAU go back a long way. deezer has the earliest release as Again on underwater in december 2004, then the Enuffs Enuff ep in 2005, and Shock To My System and Baby on etcetc in 2007 and 2008. Embrace followed in 2009 and is still the most played pnau track on last.fm.

the albums Soft Universe in 2011 and Changa in 2017 came next, the second carrying Chameleon and Go Bang, then Solid Gold and Changes in 2019, Lucky in 2020, You Know What I Need in 2022, The Hard Way, Stars and AEIOU in 2023, and the album Hyperbolic in march 2024.

the last two years are full of team-ups: The Feeling with rudimental, 1991 and ar/co on room two recordings in october 2024, Light Me Up with kungs in september 2025, a pnau remix of Parcels' Yougotmefeeling, Rollin with MEDUZA in february 2026, Just The Way You Are with milky on ministry of sound in march and Tu Corazón (Your Heart) with the warning in april. the album AHHCade came out on etcetc on 31 july 2026, followed by Nirvana with earthgang and sadmonth, Undone with the flints and Change. a DREYA V remix of Serotonin and a carter walsh remix of Tu Corazón made the friday crate along with Change, and the newest is Change (Zero Idea Remix), out on 6 november 2026.

PNAU is one of Australia’s most loved electronic acts with five critically acclaimed albums including their UK #1 album with Sir Elton John.
