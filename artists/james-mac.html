James Mac

house + afro house

James Mac is a house and afro house dj and producer, born in australia and based in france.

James Mac is a house producer with a deezer catalog that reaches back to 2015, and a lot of the recent run is with vall: I've Been Waiting with tasita d'mour on king street sounds, Work That Body, Bani Bihar on wired world, and on sweat it out The Boy Is Mine with rosalie (the top track on last.fm) and Try Again, both with a remix run.

since then: Manifest with lazarusman and Beefy on sweat it out, Breathe on robbins, Truth on insomniac, Make A Livin' with watermät, My Way on AZZUR in april 2026, the Daydream EP with watermät on siamese and Rhyme Design with watermät on club sweat on 7 october 2026.
