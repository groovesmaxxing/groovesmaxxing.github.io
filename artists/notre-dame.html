Notre Dame is a Paris producer working across nu-disco, indie dance and house, and the boss of his own label Paranormal.

He is a go-to remixer and collaborator right now: work with Bob Moses and CRi, an EP on Diynamic (No Rules), remixes for Ninja Tune and Cercle Records, and The City with Alex Wann on Armada.

Heavy rotation across the crates, with a footprint that spans juice, bumpy and wen playa.
