Torren Foot

🚧 bumpy melbourne, australia tech house

torren foot produces tech house and bass house records out of melbourne, australia. his early singles, including Be Randy in 2017 and the original version of More Life in 2019, came out through club sweat, before he moved to sweat it out for New Bottega in 2023.

a remix by John Summit brought back more life in 2020, credited to torren foot, tinie tempah and L devine and released through FFRR. it peaked at number 95 on the uk's official singles sales chart and number 88 on the official singles downloads chart, for the chart dated 12 november 2020, according to official charts.

in 2021 he added a remix to the reissue campaign for Purple Disco Machine's Fireworks, alongside reworks from breakbot & irfane and mind enterprises.

Purple Disco Machine is a funky cat! I was so excited to be offered this opportunity. As soon as I heard the boys' choir on 'Fireworks', I knew exactly what I wanted to do. I'm looking forward to seeing a crowd chanting it!

torren foot to electronic groove, 5 may 2021

his 2026 dates include a stop with QLANK at SILO in new york city on 14 august, and festival sets on 26 and 27 september at CRSSD festival's fall edition in san diego and at portola in san francisco. no colorado date is published.
