Møsard

🚧 bumpy bursa, türkiye indie dance 🎧 in rotation · 4 🧃 🚧 🌴

Møsard is an indie dance producer and dj from türkiye, and the soundcloud profile puts the base in bursa. the soundcloud bio dates the pull toward electronic music to 2015, with the first lessons at sonar house in istanbul, and says World Of Clap made the beatport hype 100.

the deezer catalog starts in november 2024 with Karna Chahta on rikodisco and counts 34 releases by november 2026. along the way: PUSH ME on sapient robots, Pressure with made in tlv on green room, the No Name EP on maneki neko, Instagram on nervous records, and the Figures and Bassline Heavy EPs with mentesh on top board and harabe black.

2026 kept the pace: All My Funk on bodega in march, Approve The Groove with ori artzi on super sapiens, FUNK on paranormal, It's Better Together 001 with niv ast, yotam russo and aviv bens on high school records, Puzzle with flamme on bassmatic, Operator on ursul, which made the top four of fresh grooves friday 011, and the Surrender EP with luca saporito on life in colour, out 16 october 2026 and in issue 012.
