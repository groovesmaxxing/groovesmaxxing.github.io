Misha Klein

🚧 bumpy indie dance + house 🎧 in rotation · 5 🧃 🚧 🌴 🏷️ label boss

Misha Klein runs headonism with deflee, and the soundcloud bio says it in three words: founder of headonism. the same soundcloud account goes by misha klein (meekky), the second name on the catalog.

the deezer discography goes back to Hands Up in september 2014, then Barbie on zmey records and Feel So Good with vika grand on lo kik records in 2015. the 2020s brought Tell Me Why on maniana records, still the top track on last.fm, The Way with no hopes, singles on pinkstar records, l music and exx muzik, and the Yellow Bones EP on tactical trax.

since late 2023 most of it lands in the family: Love The Fire, Butterflies and Chase The Sun on headonism, then Tondu, All Night and My Culture with anza on headonism noir, the Body & Mind EP with deflee, and Walked Away in july 2026. In Da Club and Hit That Rhythm with gutenn went out on headonism and radiate.
