Groove Armada

london house

Groove Armada are a duo, tom findlay and andy cato, and their own site says they first met in london back in the nineties. last.fm tags them electronic, chillout and house and counts 1,772,384 listeners, with At the River, My Friend and Superstylin' on top.

the albums run from Vertigo in 2000 and Goodbye Country (Hello Nightclub) in 2001, the one with Superstylin' on it, through Lovebox, Soundboy Rock, Black Light and White Light, Little Black Book on moda black in 2015 and Edge of the Horizon in 2020, to GA25 in 2022.

the newer records lean straight at the club. the Free Jam EP in 2024 and Remixed in 2025 came out on DFTD, Do It Right on om records in january 2026, and Love's Theme in march, with a myd remix in july. Body Action came out on shall not fade on 21 august 2026. purple disco machine remixed Paper Romance in 2025, and the newest release is a kepler. remix of Superstylin' on 2 october 2026.
