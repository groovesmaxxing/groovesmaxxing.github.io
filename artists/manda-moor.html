Manda Moor

🚧 bumpy paris, france house and tech house

manda moor is danish and filipino, grew up in france and works out of paris. she played her first big shows in 2019 and got serious about producing the same year.

I definitely got the music bug from my mother. She was a singer.

manda moor to gray area

a trip to chicago in 2019 set the direction. she met dj pierre, dj sneak, dj deeon and k'alexi there, and the 90s chicago house she took from that runs through most of what she has made since. she remixed the ron hardy memo for heattraxx on 14 june 2024 and has released on dj pierre's afro acid.

the two hot creations eps are the ones people know her for. The Climax came out on 20 may 2022 and Picante on 12 may 2023. beatport's own artist page for her reports that picante and her kaluki musik debut What U Want each reached number one on beatport's charts. she has also put records out on cecille, elrow music, paradise, sb recordings and tamango.

with sirus hood she co-runs Mood Child, described on its own site as the artistic platform of the two of them, a label, an event series and a clothing line in one. the pair also ran Mood Edits, an edit series that went twelve volumes. her own mood child records include Peligro on 28 july 2023 and Ghetto Corazon on 22 march 2024. It Looks Like Love landed on nervous records on 8 may 2026.

she plays the halo stage at creamfields in daresbury, cheshire on saturday 29 august 2026. resident advisor also has her at ants at ushuaïa in ibiza on 19 september 2026.
