Félix

🚧 bumpy uk and spain deep tech

félix is a british producer who splits his time between the uk and spain, with london as the home base. every bio on every channel he controls leads with the words self taught, and the release history backs it up, because the early records went out on small labels with nobody obvious behind them.

the first thing under the name landed on miaw on 30 march 2020, a two tracker carrying Bella Ciao and MinAnimal. The Serenade followed on andhera records on 20 may 2022. two months after that he made his kaluki musik debut with the Stand Up EP on 15 july 2022, credited then as felixo, which is how beatport still bills those two tracks.

the do not sleep connection came on 24 may 2024 with the Drop The Bass EP, three tracks deep, Colombia and Brazil Beat sitting either side of the title cut. between those two he had put Jungle Fire out on libero records on 9 february 2023, and 2 STEP arrived later on give soul records on 30 may 2025.

the biggest one is BAIANÁ, cut with the brazilian body percussion group barbatuques and released on helix records on 7 november 2025. he also has an official remix of mk's Dior to his name, and a remix of Por La Pandilla that went out on libero records on 27 march 2025.

he runs his own label and party, Offski Records, which put Dance Till You're Dead and Selecta out on 1 december 2023. bookings run through the same operation rather than through an agency.

one date is confirmed for 2026. he plays accused at eden in ibiza on sunday 23 august, on a bill with reelow, sam marzi, jamie fielding, ramin rezaie, aca and james cooper. nothing else is published on his resident advisor page and he does not run a tour page of his own.
