Kashovski

paris tech house + afro house

Kashovski is a dj and producer, and the instagram bio adds creator and electronic music digger to that. the soundcloud page lists paris as home. last.fm tags the records tech house, afro house and organic house, and the spotify catalogue goes back to Think of Me in november 2018.

Hingo with space motion came out in 2019 and The Sun Can't Compare, space motion again, in 2021, and The Same with abel ray landed on hurry up slowly in 2022. calamar records has been the steady home since, from Poison in 2022 through Pump, Burn and Gracias A La Vida with abel ray to Wally Bang in may 2026. get physical put out Bad Man in january 2024 and Force Of Nature with elliot vast in april 2026, plus two editions of get physical radio mixed by kashovski.

two came with zaro this year. Brickell came out on gozar records on 21 august and turns up in two tracklists we tagged, la siesta at playa padre and la fabrica in córdoba. Smiling followed on calamar on 9 october and went into our friday crate. Show Me with moojo came out on calamar in july.
