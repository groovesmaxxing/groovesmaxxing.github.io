Luuk Van Dijk

amsterdam tech house

Luuk Van Dijk is a dj and producer from amsterdam, which is where the bandcamp page puts home. the soundcloud bio says house scientist, and both it and the instagram bio name the label behind a lot of the catalogue, Dark Side Of The Sun, with luuk as owner. the instagram bio also names get closer, which the linktree lists with a barcelona night at les enfants, and signs off as your auntie's favourite dj.

the catalogue runs deep. the Fancy EP came out on eastenderz in 2020 and the P.O.W. EP on kaluki musik the same year, and the album First Contact landed on dark side of the sun in november 2022. Preach went out on cuttin' headz in 2023, and 2024 brought Do It Timmy on factory 93, Good 4 U with kolter on three six zero recordings and Disco Tetris on DSOTS. good 4 u and disco tetris sit on top of the last.fm plays.

the remixes go back a while, from cloonee's Sun Goes Down in 2021 to gorgon city's Run It Back on realm in 2025. Moth To A Flame with jazzy came out in october 2025. this year brought Jus Dance with mr. v and jordan brando on DFTD in january, DJ Boeie on dark side of the sun in april, Big Things Coming on love on the rocks in june, Precious Diamond with dj boring in september and Connected By Light with sam alfred and storm mollison on technicolour on 29 september.
