Syence

usa future house + dance-pop

Syence is an american dance act, filed as a group on musicbrainz, and the verified soundcloud, near 15K followers, keeps the origin story to one line. the linktree points at a curated spotify playlist, a live set from larimer lounge in denver and submissions for the act's insomniac radio show.

the deezer catalog starts with Arrival in 2017 and We Won't Leave You with steve void on lowly, then Lies on proximity in 2019, still number two on last.fm. 2021 brought hate me like u love me with jake neumar, the top track on last.fm, and the bass pop EP. after that: two armada music singles, a run on big night records, the through it all EP on TH3RD BRAIN, up all night with alex gaudino and mkla on seminal and more time on mau5trap in 2023.

the album next to happiness came out in october 2024, and since then: turn back time with nihil young and digital youth and Deep In Your Love with lea key on physical presents, feelin again and find my way on ophelia records, Crashing Down and Earth Move on lost in dreams, and Every Piece of Me with laerz on headphone society.

it all started in a science class
