NO STATIC

🚧 bumpy new york tech house 🕹️ grind mode 🕹️ frame-perfect 🕹️ unlimited ammo

NO STATIC is new york tech house, the dark kind. born and raised there, first on the scene in 2022 with Dance For Me (Family Affair), which his own bio puts past ten million streams. his beatport page lists support from FISHER, Dom Dolla, Diplo, Marshmello and Mau P, and the catalog since backs it up 🔨

the run since then reads like a label tour. Sticky on South Of Saturn in july 2025, Diva on Catch & Release in november, GENIE with ero808 and NAAR (US) on Higher Ground the same month, then 2026 opened with Pressure alongside Dansyn on IN / ROTATION, two records on GDB RECORDS (AYYBO's label), a spot on Experts Only's Excursions Vol. 6 compilation, and Digital Detox with NAAR (US) on Thrive in july.

on the Sticky premiere he told klubikon it was his most requested and anticipated song, and the demand shows in the comments every time he posts. his remixes live on soundcloud, which is where the contraband-adjacent side of the catalog hides 🏴‍☠️

this saturday he takes over a denver rooftop, and we will be on it. sleep debt entertainment's rooftop takeover at the courtyard by marriott skydeck, august 15, with Austin Feldman on direct support and Shares, Provost and Kayed heating up the floor 🤝

sets live here too. the knockdown center full set on video, then both soundcloud rides under it, including his insomniac radio hour from july 🎧
