Durante

🧃 vibes los angeles melodic and organic house 👀 one to watch · 1 🌊 ▶️ sets on file

durante is a producer and dj working in melodic and organic house. dancing astronaut called him a los angeles based artist in february 2015, when it handed him an episode of its axis mix series.

his catalogue sits across three homes. Meld, a six track record built with amtrac, elliot toller and maz, came out on OPENERS on 6 august 2021. Thread Tension followed on last night on earth on 10 september 2021. everything else runs through anjunadeep.

the anjunadeep run opens with the Celestia EP, made with HANA, on 7 may 2021. the album Enter arrived on 5 april 2024 with CRi, nathan nicholson, running touch, SOHN and HANA across its thirteen tracks, and the label handed him its twenty five year mix, 25 Years Of Anjuna Mixed By Durante, on 12 may 2025. he described the way he and HANA start a record to dancing astronaut in 2021.

Usually I'll start with a drum beat and start playing some synths while recording vocals from Hana. It's pretty free form until we get some sort of loop or melody that sticks, then we just build from there.

durante to dancing astronaut, 13 may 2021

2026 brought two more. the Prisma EP with ezequiel arias on 10 april 2026, and the How Does It Feel EP on 10 july 2026, which carries ALLKNIGHT, emi galvan and mayro across its three originals. both on anjunadeep.

the calendar runs a long way out. la biblioteca in san telmo, buenos aires on 7 august 2026, a rooftop pool party at level 8 in downtown los angeles on 22 august 2026, and tigres de la noche in washington dc on 30 january 2027. tickets are on sale for all three. no colorado date is published.
