Palms Trax

berlin house

Palms Trax is a producer and dj based in berlin, germany, in the soundcloud bio's own words. the instagram bio points at @cwpt.club, the imprint behind Petu and One Thousand Waves, and right now it says fabric presents Palms Trax is up for pre-order.

the catalogue starts with Equation on lobster theremin in 2013, still the most played track on last.fm, and Forever there in 2014. dekmantel carried In Gold in 2015, High Point on Low Ground in 2016 and To Paradise in 2019. the remixes run from mount kimbie's Marilyn on warp in 2017 to galcher lustwerk's Soul Control, erol alkan's Automatic and keinemusik's Saving My Love in 2022.

Petu with nonku phiri went out on CWPT from late 2020, then One Thousand Waves with myd in 2024 and its remixes in 2025, plus a mix for love international's sound of love international series. 2026 moved to fabric, with Soft Trance, Hard Gossip in july, Keys To A Printed Garden on 21 august, which went into our friday crate, and Kaila's Photos with mathew jonson on 14 october.
