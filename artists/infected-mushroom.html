Infected Mushroom

🚧 bumpy israel to los angeles psytrance

psytrance is not house and it is not techno, so infected mushroom sit off to one side of this page. they hold the spot on force and craft. Erez Eisen and Amit Duvdevani teamed up in israel in 1996 and put out their first album in 1999. the duo works out of los angeles.

Technology has changed immensely and we've always been into it. We're very savvy and it's important for us to be.

infected mushroom to rolling stone india, 31 january 2017

the album run is long. The Gathering arrived in 1999, Classical Mushroom in 2000, Converting Vegetarians in 2003, Vicious Delicious in 2007, Legend of the Black Shawarma in 2009, Army of Mushrooms in 2012 and Return to the Sauce in 2017. the anniversary records went to monstercat, IM25 in 2022 and IM30 on 30 january 2026, eight tracks with bliss guesting on LA Beast.

they reached number nine in dj mag's top 100 djs poll in 2007. they also build their own production plugins, and polyverse music carries them as a featured artist.

the live show runs on psychedelic visuals and a heavy stage build. dates for 2026 include summer contrast festival in poland on 4 august, sentrum scene in oslo on 18 september, alte kaserne in zurich on 19 september and dream nation festival's halloween edition at parc des expositions paris nord on 30 october.
