Fonsa

afro house + tech house

Fonsa makes afro house that leans latin and tech, going by the genres spotify files the profile under, and the records started landing in 2024. the first one on the profile is El Baile with Tom & Collins and Novak, out on the myth of nyx on 1 march 2024. the soundcloud bio says there were earlier projects too, and names talkingbodymusic and Self Distance.

klub record has been the steadiest home, from Tantas Veces with CEA and Oveja in december 2024, still the most played on last.fm, to WLU with AMO:RA, Zeitlos (CH) and V.TINO in april 2026. AMO:RA keeps coming back, on the 2 Sides release for green room in 2025 and Signs on ópalo records in 2026. there are records on SE:VER, WIRED, Madorasindahouse Records and HOTLINE too, plus a remix of My System by sentin and klub XIII soundsystem, made with ferington.

august 2026 brought Back To U with aleno, out through armada under license from unreleased records. then What She Wanted with CESHER came out on aqstica on 2 october and went into our friday crate.
