Tini Gessler

🚧 bumpy germany to sitges, spain tech house 🎧 in rotation · 4 🚧 ▶️ sets on file

Tini Gessler grew up in friedrichshafen, germany, and moved to spain, settling in sitges near barcelona, where she started playing small parties before becoming a resident dj for elrow.

I'm super proud to be a resident of elrow, but I also want to make my name as Tini Gessler.

tini gessler to mixmag, 7 february 2020

her most recent tech house singles are Play Me on LIP SERVICE in 2026 and Break on IN / ROTATION, the insomniac records imprint, earlier the same year.

resident advisor lists upcoming elrow ibiza dates for her at [UNVRS] running into the autumn of 2026.
