N2N

🧃 vibes nashville to los angeles funk house 👀 one to watch · 2 🧃 🚧 ▶️ sets on file

N2N is nick kohler, a dj and producer who grew up in nashville and has worked out of los angeles.

billboard put him among nine best sets from burning man 2018, described him as a los angeles producer working in what he calls cosmic funk, and noted he had been playing sunrise sets on the playa since 2015.

I'm forever grateful for the opportunities Burning Man has given me.

n2n to the marquee

the records carry the same warmth. Tease came out on myth of NYX on 10 october 2025, Provide, made with rob marion, followed on GDB records on 12 november 2025, and Indie, made with MNTRA and modern lover, arrived on FUNKSHWAY on 24 april 2026.

his oldest tie to anyone else on this page runs through Westend. the two of them made Retrograde, which bunny tiger released as a sharam jey edit on bunny tiger summer sampler, vol. 5 on 11 august 2017.

he also posts a numbered mix series called dance therapy on soundcloud, the sixth of which was recorded live at beach house in san diego and went up on 11 march 2026.

dates are on sale. 3OZ dive club in san diego on 21 august 2026, the barbary in philadelphia on 28 august 2026, house of yes in brooklyn on 29 august 2026 alongside the sponges, wicked woods music festival at fairmont hot springs in british columbia from 4 to 7 september 2026, and modist brewing co. in minneapolis on 11 september 2026. nothing in colorado is listed.
