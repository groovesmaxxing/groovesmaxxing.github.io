Jazzy

🧃 vibes dublin house + dance pop

Yasmine Byrne, from crumlin in dublin, and the reason a lot of records on this page have a voice on them. she arrived as a featured vocalist on belters only's Make Me Feel Good in november 2021, which reached number four in the uk and went diamond in ireland.

then she went solo. Giving Me, out on polydor in march 2023, reached number three in the uk and number one in ireland, where she was the first irish female solo artist to top the chart in fourteen years and the first to top spotify ireland since the platform launched there.

her collaborations read like a map of this page. Somedays with sonny fodera and D.O.D, july 2024, number five in the uk and fifty five weeks on the chart, with a brit awards nomination on the back of it. High On Me with Rossi. in june 2025. Invisible with Chris Lorenzo in june 2026.

If you can't play it in the club, it's not Jazzy!

jazzy to official charts, 1 june 2023

she is on chaos, polydor's dance imprint, and her debut album Peace & Patience is out on 23 october 2026. in 2025 soundcloud named her the most listened to female electronic artist in the world on the platform, and she put out a twelve track dj mix, GEWAH Selects, that october.

north america in october, dprtmnt in toronto on the ninth and village studios in vancouver on the eleventh, then belfast on 14 november and an eastenderz night at the warehouse project in manchester on 20 november alongside Cloonee.
