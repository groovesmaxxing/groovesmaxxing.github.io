Blank Sense

🚧 bumpy argentina to miami tech house 🎧 in rotation · 3 🚧 ▶️ sets on file

blank sense is an argentine tech house producer who works out of the united states. resident advisor has him born in argentina and based in the united states, and his own soundcloud page puts him in miami.

through 2021 and 2022 he worked the smaller label economy hard, with singles on high definition, goodside records, 2dutch records, candy flip, soave dusk and rule zero, several of them only weeks apart.

insomniac picked him up in 2023. Winning and Supa Dupa Type, both cut with coppa, went out on in / rotation on 8 august 2023. repopulate mars followed with the three track Monaco EP on 1 march 2024, and area10 took Hit the Brakes, made with burnr, on 22 november 2024.

the credit that carries furthest is a remix. his rework of Dollar $hort for DRAMA and flight facilities came out on drama music on 14 february 2024. he also shared a bill with Deeper Purpose on the roof at superior ingredients in brooklyn on 26 april 2025.

2025 and 2026 spread him wider. Blue Monday landed on glasgow underground on 11 july 2025 and No Fakers on thrive music on 5 december 2025. Paranoia, made with tobias gerard, went to nervous records on 6 february 2026 and Go, with the same partner, to black book records on 6 march 2026. Goddamn, cut with franco ba, arrived on diynamic's Four To The Floor 51 on 31 july 2026. a long run of his own singles sits on fugitivos, a label whose beatport catalogue is nothing but his records.

no upcoming dates are published for him. his resident advisor page carries no future bookings and its tour dates tab is empty, his insomniac artist page lists no events, and the promoters who have booked him before show nothing ahead. no colorado date is published.
