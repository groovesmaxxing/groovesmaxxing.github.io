SCRIPT

🚧 bumpy los angeles electronic + club ▶️ sets on file

SCRIPT is an electronic producer and dj, originally from los angeles going by the bio on the verified soundcloud. the same bio counts shows at the sphere in las vegas, UNVRS in ibiza and EDC in las vegas and orlando, with brooklyn storehouse, SILO dallas, concourse project in austin and empire music festival on the 2026 list.

beatport lists 19 releases, and the recent run moves fast: When It Kicks remixed for layton giordani and green velvet on madminds, Headshake on affairs, Turn Up The Dose with kream on liquid lab, a remix of zerb and rita ora's If It's Not Love, London To New York on rendezvous sound, Activator, Inside The Brain with franksy on three six zero, and Odd Boys with odd mob and goodboys on insomniac on 16 october 2026.

2025 brought Substance with alok and faangs on insomniac, Tell Me with jem cooke on easier said, Hurt / Earned Your Love on audio tactics, and a remix of maesic's Life Is Simple (Move Your Body).

a collision of indie-punk spirit, emotional songwriting, and the physical energy of the dance floor
