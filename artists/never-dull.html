Never Dull

🧃 vibes san diego disco house 👀 one to watch · 1 🌊 🏷️ label boss ▶️ sets on file

never dull grew up in san diego, california, and started producing house and disco tracks in 2017, drawing on his mexican american heritage and the city's coastal energy for his sound.

he runs his own label, Lick My Disco, and used it to put out BIG DISCO ENERGY on 23 may 2025.

in 2022 he featured on Deep Down, released 17 june 2022 by Alok, ella eyre and kenny dope on b1 recordings, and he later put out his own edit of the track.

I missed the warmth. I missed the feeling that Disco gave me back in the day.

never dull to edmnomad, 30 july 2025

he kept releasing on the label into 2026, including the track Searching.

he plays night tales in london on 12 september 2026, with no colorado dates published.
