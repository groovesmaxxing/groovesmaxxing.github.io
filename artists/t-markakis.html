t.markakis is a dj and producer based in greece, working in soulful, jazzy and deep house.

into 2026 he kept up a steady release run across labels including phoenix music, large music and opid records.

he has also put out several tracks through deepalma, including Losing Control, Higher and a remix of Inside My Head.

his single Aegean Sea came out on monophony on 3 april 2026.

he has a joint release, The Rhythm, with roland clark and Micky More & Andy Tee, due on groove culture deep on 7 august 2026.
