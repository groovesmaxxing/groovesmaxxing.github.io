Cameron Jack

london house and techno

Cameron Jack is a London born DJ and producer with releases on Crosstown Rebels, All Day I Dream and Knee Deep In Sound.

Cameron Jack is a london born dj and producer, and his own soundcloud bio says the sound came together over summers living and djing at parties in ibiza. the same bio has him playing across europe, africa, tulum and the caribbean, alongside black coffee, blond:ish, the martinez brothers and maceo plex.

deezer has the first release in april 2018, the Sans direction ep on natura viva. the abracadabra music run came next: Aye Aye with Blond:ish and ogun afrobeat in 2022, then Everything Will Be Ok, Crush and Ancestral Praise with stones & bones and reine mash. My Love For You came out on all day i dream in 2023, Red Light on hurry up slowly in 2024, now his most played on last.fm, then Just Wanna Dance on knee deep in sound and The Late Shift EP on crosstown rebels that december.

since then: a remix of Meloko's Pray for Your Love, Sixth Sense on all day i dream, Addicted on knee deep in sound, What's Up? with tamara deike on revival new york, Away From Me with sahar on when stars align, Grazie on nusonido, a remix of Mitch Oliver and might delete later's Secret on slapped and Dip For Me on soundtuary music. in august 2026 his remix of IBIZA by Tom & Collins, JADED and crcle came out on insomniac records, and The Beat EP followed on la zic on 18 september with a remix from Jeff Sorkowitz. both made the friday crate.

You can hear these strong Balearic influences in his deep and emotive sets, sounds that are echoed in his original productions.
