Toman

🚧 bumpy amsterdam minimal / deep tech 📈 heavy rotation · 8 🧃 🚧 🌴 🏷️ label boss

Toman is an Amsterdam producer working the minimal and deep tech end of house, and the boss of his own label HIGH CEILINGS.

Recent solo cuts include De La Buena, the Backstage Beauties EP, Seguimos and Emotion on HIGH CEILINGS, plus work on Cecille, NO ART and Solid Grooves.

Stripped-back, groove-heavy minimal built for long stretches.
