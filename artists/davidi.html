Davidi

either lane indie dance / tech house israel

Davidi is Itzhak Davidi, a dj and producer based in israel who lives on the indie dance side of the floor, with a tech house streak. his first record on beatport was the Backroom EP on Descending Order in april 2025, three cuts deep with Reality and Rudeboy.

since then it's Hey with TiTaN (IL) on Pure Enjoyment Black, Good Time on Collecting Dots, and a couple of edits doing laps on soundcloud: Heads Will Roll with IDAN, and his secret weapon take on We Are The People.

the records are already reaching big rooms. Adam Ten played his Ride at brunch electronik in barcelona, and Rafael played the michael jackson edit he made with Michael (ME) at playa padre. davidi named both for us himself.
