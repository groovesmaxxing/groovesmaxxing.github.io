Anyma

🧃 vibes los angeles melodic techno

Matteo Milleri, born in new york, raised in italy, based in los angeles, and one half of Tale Of Us with carmine conte. the two of them founded Afterlife in berlin in 2016, so when anyma releases on afterlife he is releasing on his own label.

the solo project has produced three albums in three years. Genesys in august 2023, Genesys II in march 2024, The End Of Genesys in may 2025, then a deluxe edition on the last day of that year which is where Dreams, made with HILLS further up this page, first appeared.

what makes him unusual is the picture. the shows are large scale audiovisual productions built around a recurring character, an android called eva, and in february and march 2025 he closed a run at the sphere in las vegas, twelve shows in all, with solomun, peggy gou, mau p and john summit guesting across the final four nights. in october he played the great pyramids at giza.

It's about the synergy or the symbiosis between humans, machines and nature. Machine learning through creativity is a positive way to bring technology and artists together.

anyma to dj mag, 19 may 2026

2026 is the ÆDEN World Tour, twelve dates announced in february, plus a tuesday residency at unvrs in ibiza running from june through to a closing party on 15 september. he finished 2025 at number ten in the dj mag top 100.

the two dates on the radar are estadio gnp seguros in mexico city on 22 august and the domain in sydney on 17 october, both part of that run.
