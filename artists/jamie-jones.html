Jamie Jones

either lane wales to london tech house 🎧 in rotation · 4 🧃 🚧 🌊 🌴 🏷️ label boss ▶️ sets on file

jamie jones is welsh and based in london.

in 2010 he started Hot Creations with Lee Foss, and the label still names the two of them as co-founders on its own site. Emerald City is his sub-label for the more song-shaped material.

The challenge of being able to pick any record and entertain people is an art form.

jamie jones to la weekly, 23 august 2012

Lose My Mind, cut with connie constance, came out on helix records on 10 march 2023, and Eats Everything turned in a remix of it on 23 june 2023. State Of Mind with camden cox arrived on the same label on 26 june 2026.

paradise is his party rather than a label. the 2026 run holds wednesday nights at UNVRS in san rafael, ibiza from 10 june to 7 october, the second summer the party has spent in that room.

those wednesdays carry on through 5, 12, 19 and 26 august 2026 and across september, with billings next to Chris Stussy on 5 august, Green Velvet and Patrick Topping on 19 august, and Prospa on 16 september. off the island he is booked for brunch electronik in barcelona on 7 august 2026 and loveland in amsterdam on 8 august 2026. nothing in colorado is on sale.
