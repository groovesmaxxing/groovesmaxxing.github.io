Seolo

🚧 bumpy budapest, hungary tech house

Seolo is a hungarian dj and producer, and the soundcloud profile puts the base in budapest. the bio there has the backstory: a former professional BMX rider, a hard backflip crash in 2016 with a serious back injury, and electronic music as the new path after it.

the deezer catalog has Quantize in 2016 and the Give Me Love EP on royalblint in 2017, and from 2021 royalblint gets a single nearly every month: The Groove Is King, Bassline King, Nein Mann, Groove Teacher, Black Betty, Listen To Gary, Feel Like Myself, We Are Playing House and plenty more.

away from it: Hot For You and Funky Mood with yvvan back on 2dutch deep and groove bassment, The Void with jack rush on wld recordings, Satisfaction and Let Me Think About It with dualities on soave dusk, Burning with maddow on uprise music and I Need Your Kiss with larakay on minute to midnite. 2026 brought Feel That Underground with titan and Move Your Body on royalblint, then Static In My Brain with sz_becca on h! music in october.

My vehicle is music. It makes me happy, it makes thousands of other people happy. I can shine my personality through music and inspire others to follow their dreams
