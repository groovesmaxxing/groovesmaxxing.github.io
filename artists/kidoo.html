Kidoo

🚧 bumpy romania minimal deep tech

kidoo is a producer and dj out of romania, working the rolling end of minimal and deep tech.

data transmission had him marked in august 2023, when MÜSE took his four track Diggy It ep. by then he had already put out music through ibiza talents and natura viva, and had debuts on solid grooves raw and mindshake behind him.

Hora De Joc landed on solid grooves raw on 13 december 2024. Luuna went to metamorfosi records on 19 december 2025, on that label's first various artists compilation. the six track Split Phases arrived on 6 march 2026.

Mezcal Bounce, cut with SOSA, came out on COCO on 15 november 2024.

the calendar for august 2026 is full. he is in the club room for joseph capriati presents metamorfosi at amnesia ibiza on 11 august 2026, a night whose terraza bill includes WhoMadeWho, and tickets are on sale. bandsintown lists further august 2026 dates in egypt, italy, ibiza, sheffield and bulgaria. no colorado date is published.
