BRN

🚧 bumpy florianópolis, brazil tech house 🎧 in rotation · 7 🚧 🎧 100K+ monthly listeners via viberate

BRN is a tech house producer and dj from brazil, and the soundcloud profile puts the base in florianópolis. the soundcloud bio lists the labels up top (solid grooves, catch & release, criterio, terminal underground, box of cats, pnk records, no bad fridays, night service only) and a long support list that runs from jamie jones, pawsa and michael bibi to mau p, john summit and chris lake.

the deezer catalog starts with Way in june 2020, then years of brazilian labels and self-released singles before the run on catch & release: Up N' Down with deeper purpose and jmnpr, Flexin with screechy, TDB, and Follower with buogo in august 2026.

Mommy landed on solid grooves records in august 2024 and is still the top track on last.fm. since then: Freak Out on 2night, Mini Skirt on terminal underground, Let Me Go with doug! and Radar with nightfunk on pnk records, Nite with marckenzo on shout label, Lose Your Mind with sorry not jomari on octopus, and One More on uprise music on 9 october 2026.
