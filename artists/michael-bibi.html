Michael Bibi

🚧 bumpy london tech house 🎧 in rotation · 4 🧃 🚧 🌴 🏷️ label boss ▶️ sets on file

Michael Bibi is a London tech house heavyweight and the founder of Solid Grooves, the label and party that helped define the modern UK sound.

His recent run reaches well beyond the underground: Feel This Grip with Baby Tate on Warner, MYSTERY OF RAW with Wu-Tang Clan and Kettama on CircoLoco, and club records like Mobile Groove on his ELOVATE imprint.

Rolling, vocal-hooked tech house made for the biggest rooms.
