KamJam

🧃 vibes tech house, indie dance 🎧 in rotation · 6 🧃 🚧 🌴 🏷️ label boss ▶️ sets on file

kamjam describes himself as a producer, dj, & multi-instrumentalist, and that is the whole bio on every profile he runs. since september 2026 he also runs his own label, playgrnd records. he is managed by arctic mgmt.

2026 is the run that put him in our crates. Superfly opened it on modern delivery on 3 july and Too Close with dario followed on all ways dance on 31 july. on 18 september 2026 he launched playgrnd records with Stay The Same, catalogue number PGR0001, cut with dario, a friend since childhood. Eternity followed on the label on 2 october.

before the label run he put out THE TIME IS NOW, LOST and HOUSE MUSIC on his own across 2025. the soundcloud side carries the remixes and edits, a 12 to 12 remix, Boogie Wonderland with dario, I Will Survive, and a take on john summit's Where You Are.

I wanted to create a home for records that feel true to me and remind me why I started making music in the first place.

kamjam on instagram, 18 september 2026, announcing playgrnd records

the same post says the label will carry his own records first and open to other artists over time. demos go to the address on the label's instagram.

his soundcloud bio lists support from zhu, augusto yepes, franc fala, kimonos, vinter, lusid and dansyn. six of them are on this site: zhu, franc fala, kimonos, vinter, lusid and dansyn.
