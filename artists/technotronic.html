Technotronic

hip house / new beat (classic 1989-90s dance)

Technotronic is the belgian electronic project that musicbrainz dates to 1988, and Pump Up the Jam is the record. it is the top track on last.fm, where the project sits at about 850 thousand listeners, with Get Up (Before The Night Is Over) and Move This behind it.

the jam keeps getting reworked. deezer lists a 2010 version with dimitri vegas & like mike, a 2018 one with coone and TNT on smash the house, the nightfunk remix in 2020 and a hugo cantarra remix in 2021, both through universal music.
