Watermät

france house

Watermät is a french house producer, and the verified soundcloud bio, past 55K followers, tells it as a decade of producing for other artists before stepping out under this name, with pete tong as an early supporter and a place on the spinnin' records roster.

Bullit in 2014 is still the top track on last.fm, which counts past 125K listeners. the deezer catalog runs through a long spinnin' deep stretch (Preach, Raise with sneaky sound system, Walls, Bring Me Back with tai and enlery, Empty), Running with raphaella on cr2, HokusPokus with moguai on punx, Blow Your Mind on solotoko, Targa and Out Of My Head on toolroom and Moonwalk Season with goldfish on spinnin'.

since 2025: Make A Livin' with james mac on sweat it out, Bounce with tai and emida on cr2, the Daydream EP with james mac on siamese, Take It Slow (Bullit) with robin schulz on spinnin' in august 2026 and Rhyme Design with james mac on club sweat in october.

I have never done anything else other than produce music and I never worked anywhere else
