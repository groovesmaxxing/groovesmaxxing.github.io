Jackie Hollander

🚧 bumpy bay area house

a product of the oakland rave scene, per her own telling, and now one of the most booked house djs in the country. dancing astronaut put her on its artists to watch list for 2025 and has called her a house and techno tastemaker.

All My Friends Are Hot came out on Nervous Records in june 2024 and became the record people know her by. since then she has released on insomniac and its in / rotation imprint, on REALM, which is gorgon city's label and further up this page, on three six zero, and on john summit's Experts Only. lp giobbi's femme house has had her too.

an earlier one, The Life, came out through insomniac's in / rotation, and she has described how quickly it came together.

I actually finished ['The Life'] in one session. If I don't bang out a song, or at least the song's template in one session, then usually I'll move on to another idea.

jackie hollander to dancing astronaut, 4 august 2024

2026 so far: People Like Us with charlie shell on REALM in february, a remix of jayda g's All Day in may, and Show Me self released in july. she played the do lab stage at coachella weekend one in april.

her diary from here reads like a groovesmaxxing shortlist. elements at pocono raceway on 7 august, a chris lake show in san jose on 22 august, fisher at unvrs in ibiza on 3 september, the experts only festival on randall's island on 19 september, hulaween in october, and rhythm and vines in gisborne new zealand on 28 december, where john summit is also billed.
