Gkat

either lane tech house united states 🎧 in rotation · 5 ▶️ sets on file

Gkat is a US tech house producer and DJ whose 2026 has been a run through the genre's tastemaker labels. In August he landed disco powder on Good Good Records, and in the spring he teamed with MAXI MERAKI for Tik Tak on Radiate Records.

Before that he featured on ROTATE VOL. 16 for IN / ROTATION, Insomniac's tech house imprint, on a deep bill of house names. The catalog leading in — Music Meets The Ocean on Queens Road Music / 10K Projects and Movin' To The Rhythm on PUTYOUON — sits in the warm, vocal-leaning end of house.

His Spotify audience is widest in Los Angeles, Mexico City and New York, with a strong Australian pull from Melbourne and Sydney. Recent sets include an IN / ROTATION x Venice Afters club set and the daytime AM.RADIO 008 house mix, both below.
