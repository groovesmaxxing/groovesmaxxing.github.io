Brad Brunner

🚧 bumpy bucharest minimal + deep tech 👀 one to watch · 2 🚧

Brad Brunner is a minimal and deep tech producer from bucharest.

Brad Brunner makes minimal and deep tech, and a lot of it lands with ag swifty. the deezer catalog starts with 009 on over records in 2017, then EPs on ole rec, tonic d, hardcutz, habla music and unnamed & unknown, Takes Over and Never Back with edip, and the Go Get It and Wahoo EPs in 2020.

take notes became a regular stop with the Step ASide EP in 2022, then Lay Back, Big Smoke's, the Handclap EP with havoc & lawn and Poppy Pill. desolat came in 2024 with Where U From with mahony and again in 2026 with 50K, alongside the Run This S EP on moan, the Shark Attack EP on coco, Breaking Bad on dosmundos and the Who 2 See EP with kov.

2026 so far: 50K, Neuromancer on short circuit, Love Back with joe vanditti on take notes trax, Poppy Pill with sapte, and Booty Call on pnk records in september. the soundcloud also carries a set from immersion festival in iceland, 3 october 2026.
