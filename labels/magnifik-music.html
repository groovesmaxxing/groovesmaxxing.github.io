Magnifik Music

BP
record label afro house melodic techno

Magnifik Music is the label Belgian DJs and producers Ajna and Samm started, co-founded in 2023 as Electronic Groove told it in june 2024. the label's own site calls itself a "record label , events and music platform", spacing and all, and says it is "dedicated to creating an inclusive and vibrant community through the power of genres like afro-house and melodic techno." the founders are Belgian and the site names no city, so neither do we. what it does name is its nights, Magnifik in Miami on 5 december 2026, London on 11 december and Antwerp on 26 december.

the about page keeps the start short: "The label’s first release dropped in early 2023, quickly followed by the release and remix of Does It Matter." Deezer dates that first release as Ajna's "On My Mind", 24 february 2023, then Samm's "Does It Matter EP" on 28 april 2023 and "Does It Matter (Ajna Remix)" on 19 may 2023, the record the about page says gained "significant attention and support from major artists like Keinemusik". Deezer files 112 albums under the label. the newest is HeAndMe's "Groove Me On", 30 october 2026, the track the label's own site embeds on its release page, a week after the "Magnifik VA" EP of 23 october 2026. asked by 6AM in june 2024 about his vision for the label, Samm put the room next to the records: "We also think it’s not only a recordl abel but more like a community where people can get involved in." spelling as written. two things share the name without being it: soundcloud.com/magnifik is a different, older account called Magnifik, and Resident Advisor's "Magnetik Music" is a different label.

the two founders lead the roster: Ajna from "On My Mind" (2023) and his remix of "Does It Matter" (2023), Samm (BE) from the "Does It Matter EP" (2023) to "Attract" (19 june 2026). Meloko is here partly as a remixer, on a remix of "Tough" with Konvex (25 july 2025), and on "End Of Time" (3 october 2025). the rest sit on their own releases or collaborations: Benja (NL) and Franc Fala share the "You Got What I Want EP" (27 february 2026), Benni Ola is on "Back Up" with HotLap, Ajna and Samm (25 april 2025), HotLap runs from "Sunbeam" (1 november 2024) to "Warehouse" (18 september 2026), Luch and Orsay share "Magik" (7 november 2025) after Luch's "FFM" (8 august 2025), MAXI MERAKI has "Look Around, It's Gonna Be Fine" (17 november 2023) and "Everybody Get Up" (24 may 2024), JUNO (DE) "Last Dance" (6 december 2024) and the "Heart Spin EP" (29 may 2026), Super Flu "Riga" (10 january 2025) and a spot on the Magnifik VA EP (2026), Raul Vidal "Lamour" on "Smile" (20 september 2024), Novak "Tu Amor" (21 february 2025), Demayä "Nothing But Love" (6 february 2026), Son of Son "Into" (20 february 2026), FENDJOU "No Seatbelt" (10 july 2026), ZARO "Pacha" (7 august 2026) and Darco "Crowd Move" (21 august 2026). 19 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Ajna, Benja (NL), Benni Ola, Darco, Demayä, FENDJOU, Franc Fala, HotLap, JUNO (DE), Luch, MAXI MERAKI, Meloko, Novak, Orsay, Raul Vidal, Samm (BE), Son of Son, Super Flu, ZARO
