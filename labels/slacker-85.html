Slacker 85

BP DG
record label

Slacker 85 is the label of Seth Troxler. Resident Advisor broke the news on 25 july 2023: "Seth Troxler has started a new label called Slacker 85." the label's Bandcamp tagline reads "From the Mind of Seth Troxler", and Discogs carries the same line. Bandcamp lists the label in Berlin, Germany. at launch, Resident Advisor quoted the label's Bandcamp page as dedicating it to "the oddball, esoteric and diverse sounds that are forthcoming from a wide range of artists and genres." that line is no longer on the Bandcamp page today. Mixmag added that "Troxler's imprint is set to encompass not only music, but fusions of art and apparel."

the idea, in Troxler's words from an Instagram post that Resident Advisor quoted: "I wanted to make something that represented me, you and everyone I know. The anti-hero, the kids who could have done it but didn't care to try. The slacker." the first release is told two ways and we print both. Resident Advisor wrote that "The first release is a four-track compilation called SLACKER001 that arrives digitally on September 1st." with Seth Troxler "Pills", Jackmaster "Let's Go", Ghoulish "Forever Ugly" and Cesar Merveille "The Finish Line" featuring André Baum. Beatport's earliest entry for the label is "Pills" on its own, SLACKER001A, dated 19 july 2023. so the single came first and the four-track record followed on 1 september 2023.

Electronic Groove wrote on 26 june 2026 that "Founded in 2023, Slacker 85 has previously featured contributions from Danny Daze, Jackmaster, Kenny Summit, and others." the same piece reported that "Seth Troxler has shared 'He Ain't Here', a new 35-track compilation out now via Slacker 85." Beatport lists 55 releases as of 17 november 2026, from 19 july 2023 to 11 november 2026. the numbered line has reached SLACKER029, with lettered parts and remix packs counted as their own releases. the newest is SLACKER029, Gaiser "What The" (Bottom Feeder, SnareBNB, Rumpty Bumpty), out 11 november 2026.

Seth Troxler is here as the founder, for "Pills" (SLACKER001A, 2023), "CircoLoco" (SLACKER020A, 2026) and a club edit of "Binman" (SLACKER016A, 2026). Lisbona Sisters are here for "Articulation" (SLACKER006C, 2024) and "U Feel Me" (SLACKER020, 2026), and Ben Sterling for "Miracles" (SLACKER026, 2026) with Deep Mariano. three are here as remixers only: Beltran with a remix of "Funky Bassline" (SLACKER015, 2026), Roman Flügel with a remix of "Just Like Magic" (SLACKER011R, 2025) and Tom Trago with a remix of Jackmaster's "Let's Go" (SLACKER004DL, 2023). 6 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Beltran, Ben Sterling, Lisbona Sisters, Roman Flügel, Seth Troxler, Tom Trago.

"My swan song, my legacy act. I've never had a label just for me."

- Seth Troxler, founder, Mixmag
