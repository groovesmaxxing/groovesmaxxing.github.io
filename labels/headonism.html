Headonism

BP
either lane record label indie dance 📈 heavy rotation

Headonism runs on a two-word bio. its own soundcloud reads "Another music by DEFLEE & Misha Klein" and nothing else. Misha Klein, who also releases as Meekky and Mad&Lynn, calls himself "Founder of @headonismrec" on his own soundcloud bio, the clearest ownership claim on file. DEFLEE is named right alongside him running the account, but no source anywhere calls him a co-founder outright, so we're not printing that title for him. neither one's real name turned up in any bio, interview or credit we checked. catalog number one, "Out of My Mind" by Deflee, Misha Klein and Fiilo (HDSM001), came out 28 may 2023. a "3 Years Anniversary" compilation (HDSM126) landed in june 2026, which lines up with that start date almost to the month.

it's the parent of a three-label family. the label's own instagram bio reads "HEADONISM | NOIR | VIBES", and this site profiles all three separately: Headonism Noir and Headonism Vibes. no source anywhere explains what actually separates their sound from the main label's beyond the names, so we're not guessing at it here. beatport tags the whole catalogue indie dance. recent singles include "Yaka" and "Under Control", both by DEFLEE and Panic Chase.

16 artists we track have released on it, listed below. the label also keeps a recurring instagram series it calls "Edits," at least four installments deep as of this writing, though instagram blocks automated reads so we could only confirm the series exists, not read the posts themselves. no location is stated for the label anywhere we could find. discogs and resident advisor both have no page for it, checked 21 september 2026.
