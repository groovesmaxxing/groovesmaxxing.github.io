AZZUR

🧃 vibes record label marseille

AZZUR has been running out of marseille since 2016, which makes it the oldest label profiled here by six years. its bandcamp says who started it and the sentence is worth reading twice, because the punctuation leaves it genuinely unclear whether two people founded it or four.

AZZUR. Marseille, France. Founded by Meloko and Konvex, Baron & Selim Sivade AZZUR has been releasing quality electronic music since 2016.

the label's own bandcamp, read 21 september 2026. the comma is theirs and this page does not resolve it

what is not ambiguous is Konvex (FR), whose verified soundcloud carries "AZZUR Founder" as his display name rather than his own. the first record, "Blooming", came out on 2 august 2016 credited to Melokolektiv and Konvex & The Shadow, which are the longer names Meloko and Konvex were using then. spotify has since rewritten those credits to the short names, deezer still shows the originals on the 2016 to 2018 records, so the same two people bookend the catalogue under four different spellings.

the only thing the label has ever said about its sound is "quality electronic music", so this page will not invent a genre for it. what the catalogue shows is a house label that leans afro and melodic, with a long habit of handing records to other people: "Me Gusta" in 2024 came back seven separate times as remixes across the following year, from AMÉMÉ, Birds of Mind, Nick Morgan and four others. four of its tracks survived the skip test this year against one the year before. seven artists we track have released on it, listed below, and the newest record is "Leave Me Dancing" by Édel, Terranova and Paul Brenning with an Nsha remix, out 18 september 2026.
