Headonism Vibes

BP
either lane record label 🌱 new

Headonism Vibes is the youngest of the three imprints under the family's shared instagram bio, "HEADONISM | NOIR | VIBES," alongside the flagship Headonism and its sister Headonism Noir. it doesn't have a soundcloud or instagram of its own yet, tracks post through the main @headonismrec account tagged "[HEADONISM Vibes]." the earliest release we found is DEFLEE and Margaryan's "Hbm", 27 march 2026, which makes the whole imprint about six months old as of this writing, easily the newest of the family. beatport's catalog prefix is VBHD, confirmed by Kila's "Split" (VBHD06, july 2026), though "Hbm" as the true VBHD01 is an inference from the numbering, not something any page states outright.

no one is named as running it, and given how new the imprint is, we're not inferring ownership from the family name alone. the catalogue so far: DEFLEE and LIINA's "Move" (august 2026) and Abel Mezzomo's "Night Call" (july 2026), on top of the two above. 4 artists we track have released on it, listed below, a small number for a label this young.

no location, no quote, no series, and no discogs, resident advisor, dedicated website or bandcamp presence, checked 21 september 2026. there simply isn't much on the public record yet for an imprint six months old.
