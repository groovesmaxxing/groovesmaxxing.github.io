Ethereal Groove Music

BP
record label afro house

Ethereal Groove Music is the record label and event platform run by Dimitris Michas. the soundcloud bio of dimitris michas says it launched in 2023, after the move to the gold coast, and dimitrismichas.com ties the label to afro house and melodic sounds.

the first release came in june 2024, Late Night by dimitris michas, numbered EGM01 on beatport. Next to You followed in september 2024 and Tuine in january 2025, then came a run through 2026: Moya in march, Buenas Noches in may, Slow with olendo in june, No Diggity with olendo in july, Heartbeat in september and Desert Light, the newest, out 29 october 2026 as EGM09. all nine are dimitris michas records and two of them carry olendo, so 2 artists we track have released on it: Dimitris Michas, Olendo.
