Not Too Fancy

BP
record label

Not Too Fancy is the label Fancy Inc presents. its instagram bio reads "Presented by: @fancyincmusic", and that is the only line the label publishes about who is behind it. it states no base, no founding date and no genre, so this page prints none of those.

the first release was Echoes of a Decade, two Fancy Inc tracks, Mad Max and Spin Back, out 27 september 2024. Electricity by Vintage Culture, Fancy Inc and Meca followed on 8 november 2024. through 2025 at least one record came out every month from january to november, closing with Fancy Inc's Brakelights on 21 november. the next one was Bop by Atta on 17 july 2026, and the newest is Find Yourself by Kobbaia, out 4 september 2026. Beatport lists eighteen releases on it as of 7 october 2026. the label's own link page also asks for demos.

Fancy Inc has five releases on it so far. Vintage Culture is here for "Electricity" (2024), ZARO for "No Time to Talk" (2025) and Knorst for "Drop It" (2025). 4 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Fancy Inc, Knorst, Vintage Culture, ZARO.
