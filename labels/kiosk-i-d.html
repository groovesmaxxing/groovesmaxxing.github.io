Kiosk I.D.

BP linktr.ee/kioskid
record label club label

Kiosk I.D. is the in-house label of Kater, the Berlin club on the Holzmarkt site. the label's Bandcamp about text reads "In-house label of Kater Club Berlin featuring club residents and assorted artists from the holzmarkt 25 universe." and its linktree keeps it to "In-house label of Kater Club Berlin!" the club was still Kater Blau when the label started, and Resident Advisor reported the rebrand to Kater on 7 august 2025, so this was Kater Blau's label at launch and is Kater's now. no source names a person behind it, so we call it the club's label and stop there. Bandcamp and SoundCloud write the name Kiosk I.D., which is how we write it, while Beatport and Deezer print it as Kiosk ID.

the name comes from a room. the first release, the various-artists "Kiosk - I.D." (KIOSKID001), landed on Bandcamp on 25 august 2019 and on Deezer the day after, and the label's own unsigned launch text on that page opens with "This is the new Kater Blau Label dedicated to our resident artists of our beloved club Kater Blau in Berlin." the kiosk was the club's hidden after-hours floor, and the text closes with "So there is no better name for our new Label than KIOSK ID !" the first artists it names are Geju, Britta Arnold, unders, WIDE AWAKE, Sven Dohse, Canson, Coss, Iorie and Christopher Schwarzwälder.

the label numbers every release, Kiosk I.D. 001 onward, and posts each one as a set on its own SoundCloud. Bandcamp lists the catalogue up to Kiosk I.D. 051 and Deezer files 57 albums under the name. the newest is Franca and Iorie's "Hypnotherapy", with remixes by Mathias Kaden and Dilby, out 9 october 2026 on Bandcamp and Deezer. along the way there were two compilations, "Kater Blau Konsortium pres. Give 'em a future" (8 may 2020) and "43 Katzen tanzen auf'm Tisch - Heinz Hopper (Pt. 2)" (1 january 2021), and vinyl, Sascha Cawa and Britta Arnold's "Good Life 25" as a 2LP on 22 august 2025. two things share the family without being the label: Katermukke, a separate label from the same club family, and KaterCast, the club's podcast on the club's own SoundCloud.

among the artists we track, Roman Flügel is here as a remixer, with his remix of David Dorad's "Murmelot" on the "Murmel EP" (2 december 2022). Adam Ten is on a club compilation, "Bedtime Melody" on "43 Katzen tanzen auf'm Tisch - Heinz Hopper (Pt. 2)" (2021). Oliver Koletzki is on a compilation and a remix, "Byron Bay" on "Kater Blau Konsortium pres. Give 'em a future" (2020) and a remix on "Dual" (18 october 2024). 3 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Adam Ten, Oliver Koletzki, Roman Flügel
