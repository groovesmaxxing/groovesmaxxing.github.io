AFFAIRS

BP DG
either lane record label

AFFAIRS is Vintage Culture's own label, launched in 2025. FrontView Magazine puts it in one line: "Founded by Vintage Culture, Affairs is a creative platform built on collaboration, emotion, and innovation." the first release is AFFAIRS001, Vintage Culture featuring Noah Kulaga "Upon Your Skin", dated 12 September 2025 on Discogs, and ONErpm's own announcement that day called it "seu novo selo", his new label. ONErpm distributes it, per that same announcement, and the label's release smart link names ONErpm as the data controller. the name comes in more than one shape. Beatport prints it "AFFAIRS", which is how Vintage Culture writes it and how we print it here. Deezer's label field reads "affairs", Discogs files it as "Affairs Records" with no profile text and no links, the Instagram account is "AFFAIRS (@our_affairs)", and EDMTunes in August 2026 called it "Affairs Music" once. no source states a base. Vintage Culture is Brazilian, but no source gives the label a city, so we don't either. the label has never written a genre line of its own, so we don't tag one. Vintage Culture's own line on the sound is the quote below.

the launch press release framed Affairs as a label and also a statement of sound, fashion and lifestyle, and the name does more than sit on the records. "Affairs" is also Vintage Culture's monthly radio show, and the show's YouTube channel description reads, verbatim: "Affairs is a monthly radio show from Brazilian house music DJ & Producer, Vintage Culture. Hear exclusive first plays of tracks from his record box, festival edits & remixes." Apple Podcasts carries it as "Vintage Culture - Affairs", with Vintage Culture listed as the publisher, and the latest episode is AFFAIRS #157, from 5 September 2026. the show ran as "Culture Shock" before it took the Affairs name, and the episode numbers carried straight on. then the club side. The Nocturnal Times reports an Affairs residency at Pacha Ibiza, every Tuesday from 9 to 30 June 2026, and an Affairs night at the reopened Pacha NYC on 3 July 2026. back on the records, the catalogue runs AFFAIRS001 to AFFAIRS018 on Discogs, the latest being Vintage Culture, Kevin Brauer and Tuggawar "Selekta EP", which Deezer dates 28 August 2026. one count does not line up. FrontView says "Lost", by Vintage Culture and Gabss, "marks the fourth release on Affairs" and gives its date as "24 December 2025", and Beatportal calls it the label's fourth release too, while Discogs files "Lost" as AFFAIRS006, dated 19 December 2025. so the catalogue numbers and the press count disagree, and we print both. either way, Beatportal's headline says "Lost" went to number 1 on Beatport.

a few notes on the names. Vintage Culture is on most of the catalogue, often as the collaborator, and the rest arrive on their own singles and EPs. Vintage Culture opens the run with "Upon Your Skin" (AFFAIRS001, 2025), "Get Naughty" (AFFAIRS002, 2025) and "Feel The Rhythm" (AFFAIRS003, 2025). Gabss is here with Vintage Culture on "Lost" (AFFAIRS006, 2025), and Max Styler with Vintage Culture and Ali Love on "Freaky 1" (AFFAIRS010, 2026). Gozlan and Kimonos are both on the "Fuego EP" (AFFAIRS004, 2025). the others: OMRI. "I Need It" (AFFAIRS009, 2026) and the "I Need It EP" (2026), Volkoder "Hands Up" (AFFAIRS011, 2026), GREG 99 "Deep Desire" (AFFAIRS012, 2026) and "Deep Desire (Extended)" (2026), Viot "Flower" (AFFAIRS013, 2026), CID "Outside" (AFFAIRS014, 2026) and Nariman "Think Too Much EP" (AFFAIRS016, 2026). also on the label, and not ours to list yet, are JØRD, Doozie, ANNA, Noah Kulaga, Ali Love, Marckenzo, Departamento, Kevin Brauer and Tuggawar. 11 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: CID, Gabss, Gozlan, GREG 99, Kimonos, Max Styler, Nariman, OMRI., Vintage Culture, Viot, Volkoder
