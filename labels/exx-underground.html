Exx Underground

BP DG
either lane record label

Exx Underground is Andrey Exx's label, and its own SoundCloud bio puts it in three lines: "Record Label, Owner @djandreyexx", "Only underground music." and "@exxboundless @exxchill @exxtalents - our sub-labels." the handle @djandreyexx is Andrey Exx, whose ADE profile places him in Moscow, and that is the owner's base, not a stated base for the label, which names none. who owns it is told two ways. the label's own bio names one owner. iHouseU, on 16 October 2023, wrote of Airsand & TuraniQa that "This couple are the owners of the top labels Exx Muzik & Exx Underground." and that "Airsand & TuraniQa is a project from Andrey Exx ... and Julia Turano (vocalist, producer and DJ)." so one owner by the label, a couple by the press, and Andrey Exx in both. Exx Muzik is the sister label, with the same owners per iHouseU, though Discogs records no link between the two. the three sub-labels from the bio, Exx Boundless, Exx Chill and Exx Talents, we found named nowhere else. Discogs keeps its profile to "Electronic music label." and notes that catalogue number EU023 was never released. no founding year is stated anywhere, so the catalogue does the talking: it starts at EU001, also numbered EXX 01, Lisitsyn "Dark Side", 2021 on Discogs and 26 March 2021 on Deezer. Discogs lists 234 releases and Deezer returns more than 300 albums under the name, so call it well over two hundred releases since 2021. no parent company or distributor turned up, so we name none. "Only underground music." is a stance rather than a genre, so we don't tag one.

the series is Best Of Exx Underground, a yearly compilation. Vol.1 (EUCOMP002, 2021) has Lisitsyn on it. Beatport carries Vol.2 (EUCOMP006, 30 December 2022), whose copy reads "Collection of the best tracks of the year from our label.", and Vol. 4 (EUCOMP016, 3 January 2025). the 2025 edition is a set on the label's own SoundCloud, embedded below. there are event compilations too: "Exx Underground ADE 2024" for Amsterdam Dance Event and "Exx Underground Miami 2025" (EUCOMP018, 28 March 2025) for Miami Music Week. no source calls any one release notable, so we list none. the newest release is Corsini "Chikaku", 18 September 2026, and it stands in below for the playlist the label does not have on Spotify. the name is printed "Exx Underground" on Beatport, SoundCloud, Discogs and Deezer, while the Instagram account displays "Exx Underground Music Label". videos go to a shared "Exx Labels" YouTube channel that also carries Exx Muzik, so it is not the label's own and is not linked here. there is no Bandcamp, no Resident Advisor label page and no website.

a few notes on the names. Lisitsyn has the first number in the catalogue, "Dark Side" (EU001, also EXX 01, 2021), plus "Zerkalo" (EXX 06, 2021) and a spot on "Best Of Exx Underground Vol.1" (EUCOMP002, 2021). ID ID is here as a remixer, on Filizola's "Moving" (2024). two rest on the label's own compilations alone: Berin on "Exx Underground Showcase ADE 2023" (2023) and Leo Sagrado on "EXXcellent Tunes Vol. 3" (EUCOMP004, 2022). SEDAT has "Emergency Exit" (EXX 80, 2022) and is on "Exx Underground Showcase ADE 2023" (2023) and "Essential Selection Ibiza 2024" (2024) as well. Gutenn pairs "Double The Sun" (2024) with "Best Of Exx Underground 2024" (2024), and PETEL pairs "Konflict" (2023) with "Best Of Exx Underground, Vol. 4" (2025). the rest are on their own releases: Benesh "Blessed" (2025), CATMOONK "It's My Life" (2024) and "Turn The Lights Off" (2026), Jon.K "Ganzi" (EXX 21, 2021) and a "Take You Home" remix (EXX 22, 2021), JONAH. "Scandal" (2024), Misha Klein "Flashback" (2024) and "We Are Free" (2025), Offir. "Daddy & Your Mind" (2024), Panic Chase "Queen" (EU043, 2022) and "Messing Around" (EU054, 2022), and Sony Vibe "Born Not To Be Quiet EP" (EXX 124, 2022) and "Born Not To Be Quiet" (2022). 15 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Benesh, Berin, CATMOONK, Gutenn, ID ID, Jon.K, JONAH., Leo Sagrado, Lisitsyn, Misha Klein, Offir., Panic Chase, PETEL, SEDAT, Sony Vibe
