AETERNA Records

🚧 bumpy record label house

AETERNA Records is MEDUZA's label, and unusually for a label this size it has said in plain words what it is for. three sources agree on who runs it: the label's own soundcloud bio opens "The record label of Meduza", discogs describes it as a house label founded by the italian trio, and the official spotify playlist sits on MEDUZA's own account. the start date is tidier than most. the first record, "Everything You Have Done (Meduza Edit)" with GENESI, landed on 10 march 2023, the youtube channel opened two days before it on 8 march, and beatport created the label entry on 27 february. three anchors inside two weeks.

the catalogue is 56 releases on discogs and it reads as exactly what the bio promises, MEDUZA records alongside records by people they wanted in the room. "Friends" in may 2023 and "Upside Down" with Poppy Baskcomb a month earlier are the trio's own. "Tides" in 2024 put them next to Beanfield and Bajka, "Melodia" in 2025 next to Argy, and ARTBAT remixed "Friends" in between. fifteen artists we track have released on it, which is the most of any label profiled here, and the reason this page exists.

Music is eternal, everlasting, and infinite. Aeterna Records is a vessel for Meduza to experiment and collaborate with their peers and document their journey, forever.

the label's own soundcloud bio, read 21 september 2026. no person is credited, this is the label writing about itself

four of its tracks survived the skip test this year against one the year before, five all-time, and four of the five sit in bumpy. the newest record is "Jump" by Maxim Lany, out 11 september 2026, which both deezer and the label's own playlist description confirm.
