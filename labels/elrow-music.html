elrow Music

BP DG
record label tech house

elrow Music is the record label of the elrow party brand, putting out records since 2012. the parties, the festival stages and the YouTube channel belong to the brand, and this page is about the records. Gray Area, writing in 2022, put the start this way: "So it only makes sense that the brand expanded into the record business with the founding of elrow music in 2012." the same piece adds: "When the label was created in 2012, the main objective was simply to release music from the brand's residents." the label's own SoundCloud bio keeps it short: "Electronic music label focused in tech-house sounds since 2012. We also like confetti & inflatables". Discogs files it as a "Techouse & Techno label from Barcelona, Spain." the label's own pages name no city.

as of 2022 De La Swing ran it. Gray Area wrote then that "As the head of elrow music for almost nine years, De La Swing has witnessed wave after wave of change within the genres that elrow music represents." in 2018 the A&R was Baum, who told Inflyte that "De La Swing and I have our studios in the same building and we work day to day in the label and in our own music." Beatport dates the first release, Elrow Music Collective 01, to 23 may 2012, and as of 17 november 2026 it lists 279 releases under the catalogue prefix ERM, the newest ERM285. in 2019 the label announced a sub-label, elrow Limited, which Gray Area says "focuses exclusively on deeper, more minimal sounds." Discogs lists it as a sub-label too. its releases are not counted on this page.

the backbone is the numbered various-artists series, elrow music 20xx. the newest is elrow music 2058 (ERM285, 13 november 2026), with Amedeo Picone "Sax Twist", Davide T and Muslija "No Photos", Mr.Martin and Alex Atenciano "Dance 4 Me" and Toomy Disco "Nothing to Hide". the one before, elrow Music 2057 (ERM275), is dated 30 april 2026 on the label's SoundCloud. older in-house compilations sit alongside it, Elrow Music Collective 01 from 2012 and event-tied ones such as Miami Off 2017 Elrow, ERM100 on Discogs.

Dennis Cruz is here for "Tarantula" and "Cala Bassa" (2015), plus a remix in 2019. Wade is here for "Specimen" and "Acidness" (ERM086, 2016) and "Bump N Grind" (ERM118, 2017), Solardo for "Not Over" (ERM090, 2016), Cloonee for "Estes", "Like This" and "Haah" (ERM103, 2017) and more after, and CamelPhat for "Bang 2 Drum" and "World In Action" (ERM117, 2017). Iglesias is here for "Been A Long Time" and "Disco Music" (ERM185, 2020), Shermanology for "Souljack" and "Da Beat" (ERM216, 2022), Andruss for "Tomala (Se Mueve Se Siente)" (ERM247, 2024) and Tony Romera for "McLovin'" and "La Funk" (ERM280, 2026). the remixers: Sidney Charles on "In Time" (ERM108, 2017), Franky Rizardo on "Body Jack (Get My Swerve On)" (ERM131, 2018), ANOTR on "Flown Alone" (ERM144, 2018), Jansons on "Atomic Fireblast" (ERM169, 2019), ALISHA on "Badd" (ERM218, 2022) and Manda Moor on "Cymbal" (ERM256, 2024). 27 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: ALISHA, Andruss, ANOTR, Bruno Furlan, CamelPhat, Cloonee, Crewcutz, Dennis Cruz, Eddy M, Fancy Inc, Franky Rizardo, Iglesias, Jansons, Jay de Lys, Manda Moor, Marco Strous, Martin Ikin, Mikey Lion, NightFunk, Piem, RUZE, Shermanology, Sidney Charles, Sllash & Doppe, Solardo, Tony Romera, Wade.

"As I said before, for us the key is to be constant and offer a quality product: sometimes bangers for the dance floor, sometimes timeless music but always good stuff."

- Baum, elrow A&R, Inflyte
