1001 Recordings

BP DG
record label

1001 Recordings is a joint venture between 1001Tracklists and Tomorrowland. its own about page calls it "a groundbreaking new record label with the dancefloor at its heart", and its site carries the line 1001 Recordings by 1001Tracklists & Tomorrowland Music. EDM Identity reported the launch on 4 july 2025. the label names no base and no founder, so this page prints neither.

the same page promises that "every release will be dancefloor-ready, built to make an impact at both festivals and nightclubs worldwide." the first release was Take Me There by Sick Individuals, Matisse & Sadko and Third Party, out 11 july 2025, and Dillon Francis and MARTEN HØRGER's B2U EP followed on 18 july. the label's site lists 24 releases as of 9 october 2026. the newest are seven tracks out that day, which Beatport also sells together as the compilation FLOORWORK VOL. 1. the site also takes demos.

Rafael Cerato is here for "Sound Vibrate" (2025), with Wave Wave, and for "Pink P" (2026). Blank Sense is here for "Drum Check" (2025), WELKER for "Tussy" (2026), DANSYN for "Step To The Floor" (2026) and Amour Propre for "Stay Low" (2026), one of the seven FLOORWORK tracks. 6 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Amour Propre, Blank Sense, DANSYN, Rafael Cerato, Wave Wave, WELKER.

"For years, we’ve kept our finger on the pulse of dance music, and the launch of 1001 Recordings was a natural step to give a bigger spotlight to the artists and music our community already believes in and champions."

- Jacob Merlin, CMO of 1001Tracklists, EDM Identity
