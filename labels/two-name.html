TWO NAME

BP
record label

TWO NAME is the name on the label page at Beatport and in Deezer's label field on 167 albums, and that is close to everything anyone has written about it. no source names a founder, an owner, a home base or a founding year, so none of that is on this page. what the catalogue shows is singles, lots of them, and fast. Deezer lists several every week in october 2026, three on 23 october and three more on 30 october, the newest of them Afilso Good by Clim and Tineway. the Beatport page has Mandala by THE NORTH and BORIQUE, Code Breaker by Meriva and Lapua (BR), Techno Cabron by Avi Murz and Say Yes by MoniK (RU) all up on pre-order, with All Night by MOSK (IL), Moving Like a Ghost by Baldo Riva, Acid by Sulim and Dr.Luxe and Om Namah Shivaya by Javica out now. the label has never written a genre for itself, so we don't tag one.

the catalogue on Deezer starts in earnest on 18 april 2025 with Ngani by Borsky. one oddity sits before it: Deezer dates a single, Moans, to 1 march 2024, a full year ahead of the rest. that is a date on one record, not a founding date, so we print it and leave it there. the label also runs its own compilation line, the seasonal Session compilations, various artists, from Winter Session 009 (Two Name) on 16 may 2025 through Summer Session 015 (Two Name) on 27 september 2026, ten tracks with Juskidin among them.

one more thing Deezer's data shows, printed as an observation and nothing more. the label field reads UNCLES MUSIC COLORS on Heart Beat by DR:EW (11 april 2025) and on the compilation Autumn Session 008 (Uncles Music Colors) (20 december 2024), then TWO NAME from Ngani one week later and on Winter Session 009 (Two Name). the Session numbering runs straight on across the two names. Deezer's data reads like a rename. no source says so, and we draw no conclusion past that.

both names we track are here on their own singles, Borsky from the very first one. Borsky has Ngani (18 april 2025), Games (2025), tracks on the Session compilations and Promises (18 september 2026). Juskidin has Give It to You (24 july 2026), All Night (14 august 2026) and a track on Summer Session 015 (27 september 2026). 2 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Borsky, Juskidin
