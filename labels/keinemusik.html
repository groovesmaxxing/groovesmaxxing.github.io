Keinemusik

BP DG RA
either lane record label house · techno

Keinemusik is the Berlin label and crew that &ME, Adam Port, Rampa and Reznik founded in 2009. the label's own Bandcamp bio puts the whole idea in one breath: "Keinemusik is a network with the label as the pulse pumping centre, driven by the spirit behind the collective: to find your own agenda, refine House and Techno off the beaten path, and build recall across music, art, and image. Founded in 2009 by &ME, Adam Port, Rampa and Reznik, it spans releases, global parties, TEILE, and Worldwide Dancing Club." the crew page on keinemusik.com keeps it to "Keinemusik has been around since 2009." and names the same four as its members. how many people that makes depends on who you ask. the label's own words count four founders. Discogs names five people running it, Rampa, &ME, Adam Port, Reznik and Monja Gentschow, and files Monja Gentschow as an illustrator. Beatportal's 2019 feature by Alice Austin says "The five new friends" formed Keinemusik in 2009. four or five, then, depending on the source. the base is Berlin, per the label's Bandcamp location, Resident Advisor and the Discogs profile, which reads "Electronic music label based in Berlin, Germany." the name is printed "Keinemusik" on its own site, Bandcamp, Beatport, Resident Advisor and Discogs alike, and the label code is LC 35439. the first catalogue number, KM001, is David Mayer "Nacktenordner EP" (2009) on Discogs, and Deezer's earliest is &ME "F.I.R." (KM002), 5 May 2009. Discogs also lists a sub-label, keinerechte, whose releases do not count toward the roster here.

the genre tag is the label's own phrase, "refine House and Techno off the beaten path", so house and techno. the collective and the label share the name, and Keinemusik plays as a crew and throws parties as well as putting out records. the crew page says of the output that "A more or less constant count of five releases per year have been released ever since, solely laid down by members of the crew." that last clause is worth holding against the catalogue, and the roster paragraph below does. the series is the Keinemusik Radio Show, a set on the label's own SoundCloud, linked from the "show radio" button on keinemusik.com. it is live, with guest hosts and new episodes through 2026. SG Lewis hosted the episode of 1 May 2026, and the latest at the time of writing is by Bontan, 11 September 2026. on the records, EDM Sauce calls Adam Port, Stryv and Malachiii "Move" (2022) the label's commercial monster, number 1 in several countries, and names the &ME, Rampa and Adam Port records "Muyè" (2017) and "Confusion" (2021) alongside it. the newest release is Adam Port and SG Lewis "Be The One" (KM075, 24 April 2026). one thing that is not the label: Drake's "Falling Back" and "A Keeper" (2022) were made with &ME and Rampa, and the label's site blogged it, but they came out on OVO Sound and Republic. a few notes on the names, and on that "solely laid down by members of the crew" line. every release we can see carries a crew member, and outside names arrive as remixers or next to a member. &ME, Adam Port and Rampa are the founders on their own records, from &ME "F.I.R. / +++" (KM002, 2009), Adam Port "Chemistry" (KM004, 2009) and Rampa "Wife & Man" (2009), through "Workparty One" (2010), which carries all three, to Adam Port "Corrosive Love" (KM009, 2011) and Rampa "Workparty Two" (KM010, 2011). Keinemusik, the crew itself, is a credited artist on its own albums, "You Are Safe" (KM039, 2017), "You Are Safe Remixes" (KM040, 2018) and "HIGHSNOBIETY 20190801" (2019). four are here as remixers. Black Coffee (KM041), DJ Tennis (KM040) and Solomun (KM042) all have "You Are Safe" remixes from 2018, Black Coffee also has "Muyè (Black Coffee Remix)" (2018) and is on "The Rapture Pt. III" (KM066S1D, 2023) with &ME, Solomun also has "Send Return" (KM059, 2022), and Bedouin, the Brooklyn duo, has a "Doppelgänger 01" remix (KM038, 2017). three are collaborators with Adam Port: SG Lewis on "Be The One" (2026), Stryv and Malachiii on "Move", which Beatport dates 2022 and which Discogs files as a 2024 release listing Keinemusik and Interscope Records together, with Camila Cabello among the credits, so a joint release. the one light contradiction sits at the very start of the catalogue: KM001 is David Mayer "Nacktenordner EP" (2009), and David Mayer is not among the four founders, though he plays in Keinemusik line-ups. Reznik, the fourth founder, is not on our roster yet. 11 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: &ME, Adam Port, Bedouin, Black Coffee, DJ Tennis, Keinemusik, Malachiii, Rampa, SG Lewis, Solomun, Stryv
