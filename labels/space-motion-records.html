Space Motion Records

BP DG
record label

Space Motion Records is the imprint discogs files as the label of dj and producer Space Motion, founded in 2018, and his own tiktok bio names it right under dj and music producer. the label names no base of its own. his soundcloud lists novi sad, serbia.

the catalogue runs on SMR numbers. discogs starts it with Freak as SMR001 in 2018, then Bombaya and the Esto Es Heaven EP. by 2019 and 2020 friends were on it too, Kashovski on Hingo and the Yana / Hayati EP, Stylo on the Amazon Rise EP and the Space Friends releases, and Tomi H on Vuka with Oluhle. beatport's label page was up to SMR100DJ when we read it on 2 november 2026.

the pace picked up in late 2025. spotify's label search finds 18 releases between october 2025 and october 2026. Space Motion is on most of them, and other names carry their own, Junior Pappa's Mars EP, Addie Manson's Afterhours and Rebirth by FiNE and ANTICALM, the newest, out 2 october 2026. Soofnic joined him for the Loopy Loopy EP in september. 3 artists we track have released on it: Soofnic, Space Motion, Tomi H.
