DEEPERFECT

BP DG RA
either lane record label tech house · minimal deep tech

DEEPERFECT is the Florence label Stefano Noferini founded. the Italian DJ and producer calls it "my label", and the site footer reads "© 2022 Deeperfect Records - Florence Italy". Bandcamp gives the location as Italy and the Discogs profile calls it an "Italy based house and tribal house label." the name is written three ways, all of them the label's own: "Deeperfect" on its Beatport page and on Bandcamp, "Deeperfect Records" in the site footer and on Resident Advisor, and "DEEPERFECT" on Instagram and on its Spotify playlist. we go with the last one, the wall's spelling. Discogs does its own thing and files the label as "Dee-P-erfect", with a separate "Deeperfect Records" entry that its own profile says is only for copyright lines. the start date comes in more versions than the name, and we print them all rather than pick one. the label's Bandcamp bio says 2003, "Record Label born in Italy in 2003 Tech House, Minimal Deep Tech.", and Ibiza Spotlight and Wikipedia say 2003 as well. Resident Advisor's label page says established 2004. the catalogue says earlier still: DPE 002, Exelent Trite "Congenial EP", is dated 2001 on Discogs, and so are DPE 003 to DPE 007. then there is a compilation called "Ten Years of Deeperfect Records", out on 11 May 2010, which counts back to about 2000. four answers, all sourced, unresolved.

Discogs lists the parent as Sound4Group, and that is as far as we take it. from 2010 the label worked with Self, a large Italian distribution company, per Beatportal's Label of the Month piece in January 2026. Discogs also shows two sub-labels, DEEPERFECT Shift and DPE, whose releases do not count toward the roster here. the sound in the label's own words is brief: "Tech House, Minimal Deep Tech." in the Bandcamp bio, "Bringing you the Italian touch with perfection. Deep Tech: Deeperfect" on the Spotify playlist, and "GET DEEP, AIM FOR PERFECT" as the site tagline. Noferini put it to Beatportal in January 2026 as "It's always the groove first...because it's the main thing that I want to hear from the track, and it's what our sound is based on." Resident Advisor's own bio for the label, RA's line and not the label's, reads "Deeperfect Records is one of the most important Italian Label." the label also runs a production school, Deeperfect Academy. on the catalogue: Exelent Trite "Congenial EP" (DPE 002, 2001) is the earliest catalogue number on Discogs, UMEK & Stefano Noferini "Goes On" won Best Minimal/Techno Track at the 2013 International Dance Music Awards, "Ten Years of Deeperfect Records" is the 2010 compilation, and "Deeperfect Records #Beatportdecade Techno" (DPE869, 12 September 2014) was the label's part of Beatport's tenth-birthday series.

the radio show is Deeperfect Radio Show, a set on the label's own SoundCloud, and the label site links it as its radio show. Club Edition is a separate thing: Noferini's own weekly radio show, which Beatportal in January 2026 said goes out on 110 stations across 15 countries, and an older set titled "Stefano Noferini Presents Club Edition Summer 2018" sits on the label account too. on the party side, Deeperfect at Eden, Ibiza ran eight Wednesdays from 23 April to 11 June 2025, billed by Ibiza Spotlight as the label's longest series of parties to date, and Deeperfect x Eat The Beat Club Train, a joint party with the Melbourne promoter Eat The Beat, was last held at ADE in Amsterdam on 24 October 2025. a few things share the name without being the label: the Discogs artist "Deeperfect", and the labels Deeper Rekords NYC, Perfect Records and D.E.P. Records, are all unrelated.

a few notes on the names. six are here as remixers: Ben Sterling on "What I Do" (DPE1802, 2021), Brad Brunner on "Blueberry Brownie EP" (DPE1899, 2022) and "Metamorfosis" (2024), German Brigante on "The Specialist" (DPE589, 2013), Max Dean on "Time EP" (DPE1849, 2022), and Balanka on "Tasty" (2026) and Gio Lucca on "Block" (2025). three rest on compilation tracks rather than their own releases: Aitor Astiz and Bruno Bona on "Winter Club Cuts 2025" and TWOLATE (ITA) on "Summer Cuts 2026". Trace is here for "Dhamar" (2024) and "We Gonna Do" (2026). the rest are on their own releases, from Dennis Cruz's "A Better Day" (DPE982, 2015) and Jean Pierre, on "Deeperfect Ibiza 2016 Mixed by Elio Riso" (2016) and then his own "Just Before Loco Ep" (DPE1625, 2019), to Justin Rabin's "Onda" and RSquared's "Aeroplane Mode", both 2026. 29 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: AG Swifty, Aitor Astiz, Balanka, Ben Sterling, Brad Brunner, Bruno Bona, Dennis Cruz, Discip, EdiP, FIRZA, Funk Cartel, German Brigante, Gio Lucca, Iglesias, Jay de Lys, Jean Pierre, Jeff Sorkowitz, Justin Rabin, Kidoo, Mason Collective, Max Dean, Piem, Ranger Trucco, Roddy Lima, RSquared, Trace, TWENTY SIX, TWOLATE (ITA), Vanny Granata
