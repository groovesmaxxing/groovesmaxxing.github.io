Top Board

either lane record label tel aviv

Top Board has released steadily since january 2025 and has never once put a name to itself. the imprint line on every record reads Top Board, not Top Board Records, which is what its handles say. its bandcamp carries the only self-description it has written anywhere, "Top Board. Israel. Fresh and new music label From Tel Aviv." no source names a founder, a label head or an A&R, and the one page most likely to carry one, the demo intake at tstack.app, refused every request on 21 september 2026. so no owner is printed here.

no founding year is stated either, so the catalogue stands in for one. the first release is Full House VA1, seven tracks, 17 january 2025, and the youtube channel was opened on 22 october 2024, roughly three months ahead of it. the label has never written down what it sounds like, and its beatport page carries no description, only the boilerplate beatport prints on every label. the catalogue says it plainly enough: it runs on compilations and on singles in bulk. on 18 september 2026 it put out Full House VA4 and nine separate singles on the same day.
