Bona

BP
record label

Bona is the label of Bruno Bona, a DJ and producer from La Serena, Chile. his own Beatport bio calls him "Founder of his own label bona", and the label's SoundCloud, where it goes by bona label, reads "Since 2024 by @brunobona_". the same SoundCloud bio reads "Label & Party Events", so the label describes itself as a party name as well as a record label. it states no base of its own, so La Serena is where its founder is from, not an address for the label.

the first release was Sunset EP by Bruno Bona (BON001), out 16 february 2024. Beatport counts 65 releases as of 17 november 2026, and the numbered line has reached BON058. that newest one is What It Is EP (BON058, 13 november 2026), two tracks led by Rizzo (Col), "What It Is" with Bruno Bona and "Ay Muchacho" with Carduss. Bruno Bona is credited on about half the catalogue. Truelove Music's profile of him goes further. it says he has positioned the imprint "among the top-selling labels worldwide" and credits it with a Beatport #1 for "Sexy Back" (BON016, 2025). that is Truelove's claim, and we print it as theirs.

Bruno Bona is here as the founder, on the first release with "Sunset" and "Move That" (BON001, 2024) and on about half the catalogue since. Esteban Angel is here for "Sexy Back" (BON016, 2025). 2 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Bruno Bona, Esteban Angel.
