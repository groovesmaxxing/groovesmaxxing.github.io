Headonism Noir

BP
either lane record label indie dance

Headonism Noir is the second of three sister imprints, alongside the flagship Headonism and the newer Headonism Vibes. the family shares one instagram, its bio reading "HEADONISM | NOIR | VIBES," but Noir runs its own beatport catalogue (prefix NRHD) and its own soundcloud. no source anywhere says what makes "Noir" sonically different from the main label, so we're not inventing a distinction that hasn't been stated. the earliest release we could confirm is LIINA's "You and Me" (NRHD02), 3 january 2025. the catalogue's actual NRHD01 didn't turn up in any search, so an exact launch date is unconfirmed, though the numbering puts it close behind.

no one is named as running Noir specifically. Headonism's own founder, Misha Klein, claims that title for the main @headonismrec account and brand by name, not for Noir, so we're not extending it here on association alone. the roster runs through some of the same names as the flagship, DEFLEE most often, with "Shake" (NRHD06, july 2025) and "Say Something" (february 2026). Kolombo's "Keep Fighting" came with a remix package from Heartless House, Novikov (ofc) and Panic Chase in may 2026.
