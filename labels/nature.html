NATURE

BP DG
record label

NATURE is the label wAFF, a UK house and techno producer, launched in 2021. Beatportal broke the news on 5 november 2021 and called it "his new environmentally focused music label, Nature." Electronic Groove reported the launch again on 7 february 2022. the name is printed NATURE on Beatport and on the label's Instagram, Beatportal and Discogs write Nature (Discogs files it as Nature (10)), and Deezer's label field reads Nature Records. the label states no base, so this page prints none.

wAFF told Beatportal "Nature is so important to me, so I wanted something that felt like an extension of myself and what I care about so much." the same launch piece said "wAFF's Nature imprint will work to donate a portion of its profits to various animal charities with every release." that is the aim the label set out with in 2021. the dates do not line up. Beatportal said the launch record, the Colours EP, would come out on 26 november 2021, and Beatport dates Colours (NATURE001) 4 february 2022. so the label was announced in november 2021 and its first record is dated february 2022. Discogs lists a vinyl pressing of it.

Beatport lists 29 releases as of 17 november 2026, NATURE001 to NATURE029. wAFF took the 12th himself, Leaving You (NATURE012), which Discogs lists under both 2024 and 2025, and the vinyl shop Global Groove still calls him "the Nature head" on its listing for it. the newest is Nobody by Pako Ramirez (NATURE029), out 13 november 2026 with "Realise" and a Ssero remix of the title track. FIRZA is here for NATURE016 (2025), three tracks with "Let's Get Scratchin'", "Get Up, Dance" and "Orb Control", and Funk Cartel for NATURE011 (2024), three tracks with "Imprints", "Incognito" and "Stick Together". 2 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: FIRZA, Funk Cartel.

"Nature heals us, and that's something I like to think of with this label."

- wAFF, founder, Beatportal
