Reprieve Records

BP reprieverecords.com solo.to/reprieverecords
record label denver

Reprieve Records is the label gs5 runs. its own soundcloud reads imprint by @gs5_music and lists denver as home, and gs5's soundcloud calls him the label boss.

the catalogue is four numbers so far. RR001 was the Wonky Tech EP by jezz and gs5 in march 2026. RR002, the Reprieve Summer Sampler, came out in july with seven artists on it, londen summers with gs5 on De With It and matt egbert's Pacifico Pat among them. RR003 is The Goods EP by matt egbert and gs5, out 2 october, and both its tracks went into our friday crate. RR004, the Hypnotise EP by J6, is up for preorder on beatport for november.

there is a radio show too. Reprieve Records Radio runs on gs5's own soundcloud, eleven episodes in by october, the newest with matt egbert.
