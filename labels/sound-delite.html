Sound D'Elite

BP
record label

Sound D'Elite is Ilario Alicante's label. Electronic Groove reported the launch on 9 february 2024, writing that "Italian DJ and producer Ilario Alicante has expanded his musical project with the launch of a new label called Sound D'Elite." it sits beside Virgo, his techno label, and in the same piece he put the pair this way: "These labels are like two parallel narratives that, while following different paths, converge in drawing an authentic and multifaceted portrait of who I am as an artist." the label states no base and names no parent or distributor, so this page prints none.

the first release was Wish Star, a three-track EP by Joe Vanditti, SDE001, which Beatport dates 9 february 2024. by 10 september 2024 When We Dip was premiering Thomas Cerutti's five-track Louder EP "for the label's tenth release." that is ten releases in about seven months. Beatport lists 50 releases as of 17 november 2026, SDE001 to SDE050, and the newest is SDE050, Mahony's Sex Appeal with "Moog", out 13 november 2026. in june 2025 the label opened a various-artists compilation series, Elite Gathering. FindYourSounds called it "the inaugural chapter of a brand-new VA series, timed to coincide with Sonar 2025 festival", and wrote that Elite Gathering Vol.1 (Sonar 2025 Edition) "drops via Sound D'Elite on 13th June 2025." only volume one is confirmed.

Tony Romera is here for "Can't Sleep", "Now Do It" and "Este Mambo" (SDE040, 2026), FIRZA for "Fever", "Sirens" and "Bounce" (SDE039, 2026) and Kidoo for "Poem" and "In Transit" (SDE042, 2026). Braydon Terzo is here for "Work" and "Keep It Real" (SDE029, 2025) and EdiP for "Lock Y Phone" and "I Know U" (SDE027, 2025). Justin Rabin is here for "Handle This" and Vanny Granata for "Two Times", both on SDE021 (2025). Iglesias has "Pills n Thrills" and "Pluck Off" (SDE031, 2025) plus a remix of "Never Sax Enough" (SDE028, 2025), and YOUniverse (IT) is here as a remixer only, with a remix of "Pluck Off" (SDE031, 2025). 9 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Braydon Terzo, EdiP, FIRZA, Iglesias, Justin Rabin, Kidoo, Tony Romera, Vanny Granata, YOUniverse (IT).

"With Sound D'Elite, I showcase a groovier and more housey side of my style that, although always present, now takes center stage..."

- Ilario Alicante, founder, Electronic Groove
