Solid Grooves Raw

DG
record label sub-label of solid grooves

Solid Grooves Raw is the sub-label of solid grooves. discogs files it under Solid Grooves Records with SGRAW catalogue numbers, and the copyright lines on its recent records read Solid Grooves Records too. the first record found is ramin rezaie's Jibberush EP from october 2017.

the solid grooves founders were on it early. PAWSA's Get Down EP and michael bibi's Youth Culture EP came out in spring 2018, bibi came back with Frequency in 2019 and Lemonade in 2020, and PAWSA with Hypnotic Dub in 2021. cloonee's The Ciggie landed in december 2018, dennis cruz's Better Than Pussy in 2019 and SOSA's Don't Mess With The 808 in 2020.

the Raw Grooves compilations run through the catalogue, up to vol. 7 in april 2025 with kidoo and tony romera on it. the singles since include records from sepp, djebali, laidlaw, obskür, prodot and JUST2, then crewcutz's Disco Jungle on 11 september 2026 and reboot's Haha on 9 october, which went into our friday crate.
