LTF Records

RA DG linktr.ee/ltfrecords
record label amsterdam, netherlands

LTF Records is Franky Rizardo's label. its soundcloud bio puts it plainly, "Franky Rizardo proudly presents LTF Records." the letters stand for listen to flow, and the label pitches itself as a home for his own records and a way to pull listeners into that state of flow. its instagram calls it a record label by franky rizardo and listen to flow.

discogs files it as a dutch label founded in 2020, and the earliest release we found on it is his own Primrose EP, april 2020. bandcamp places it in amsterdam. these days it puts out something most fridays, from franky's Marido Dansa EP on 2 october to Night Mode by FIRZA on 9 october. the label's resident advisor page says it made beatport's top 10 best-selling house labels of 2025, and its linktree is selling tickets to an LTF label night at ADE.
