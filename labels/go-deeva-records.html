Go Deeva Records

DG godeeva.com
record label italy

Go Deeva Records is the house and tech house label discogs files as based in italy and run by simone vitullo, and simone vitullo's own records keep turning up on it, from Dope Your Mind in 2015 to Rapture in october 2026.

the catalogue is deep. discogs lists 552 releases under it, plus three sub-labels, go deeva light, go deeva classy records and go dooria records. the oldest record deezer carries under the name is the Never EP by alex gori and imerio vitti, from december 2011. there are compilation series too, the numbered FIVE TO BE RIGHT various artists sets, up to 23 in september 2026, and city editions like Go Deeva Miami 2017.

a lot of names we track came through it. DJ PP had El Sol (Remixes) in 2013, the year of Volkoder's Detroit EP, and Fancy Inc followed in 2015 and 2016. Piem put out Stucked in Ny with yamil, which Dennis Cruz remixed, Rafael Cerato did Better Way with vanilla ace, and David Penn remixed simone vitullo's My Darling. Notre Dame had Recital and Timeless in 2021, MAXI MERAKI made Tsona with idd aziz in 2022, JUNO (DE) landed Reverse in december 2024 and Sllash & Doppe Guadalajara in 2025, and Nico de Andrea remixed Man With The Red Face that december. october 2026 brought Gimme Love by Konstantin Kobra and Move Your Body by Meer (IT), and both made our friday crate. 13 artists we track have released on it: David Penn, Dennis Cruz, DJ PP, Fancy Inc, JUNO (DE), Konstantin Kobra, MAXI MERAKI, Nico de Andrea, Notre Dame, Piem, Rafael Cerato, Sllash & Doppe, Volkoder.
