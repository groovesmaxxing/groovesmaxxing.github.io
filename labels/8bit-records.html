8bit Records

RA DG
record label tech house

8bit Records is a tech house label from mannheim, germany, run by nick curly and gorge. that is how both resident advisor and discogs describe it, and resident advisor dates it to 2004.

it started out as amused 8bit, with the same AMR catalogue numbers as amused records, and discogs opens the catalogue with nick curly's E.P as AMR001 in 2004. the discogs profile says the label moved to intergroove for distribution in 2006, switched to 8bit catalogue numbers and has been 8bit records since.

the back catalogue has records from Sidney Charles, Josh Butler, Jansons and Franky Rizardo, whose Dusty Mornings came out on it in november 2020. the founders still put out their own, nick curly's Bahia EP in june 2026 and gorge's Musica EP on 30 october 2026. from june to november 2026 deezer lists a release about every two weeks, among them the Questions EP by alan fitzpatrick, Piem and slm in august, Across The Room by malikk in october and Mother by paisley jensen, the newest, out 13 november 2026. earlier in the year Qubiko had the Momento EP in january and Trace had Good Time in february, and Kuuda were on it in may 2024 with Third Room, made with keeq. 9 artists we track have released on it: Franky Rizardo, Jansons, Josh Butler, Kuuda, Piem, Qubiko, Sidney Charles, Solomun, Trace.
