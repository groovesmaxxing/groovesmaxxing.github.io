Collecting Dots

WWW BP
either lane record label house + tech house 📈 heavy rotation

Collecting Dots is OMRI.'s label, curated by him per the site's own footer, and named as his in every piece of press covering the launch. it debuted 31 january 2025 with his own single, "Mission Impossible" (CD001). the site's own footer copyright oddly reads "© 2024," a year off from every dated press source, a stale template default rather than a real second founding date, so we're going with the well-sourced january 2025 figure. no formal headquarters is stated for the label itself, OMRI. is repeatedly described as Tel Aviv-born and part of that scene, which the label's own launch copy leans into directly.

Starting my own label isn't just a step; it's a leap into the unknown, an evolution of everything I've poured into the music scene of Tel Aviv and beyond.

OMRI., on launching Collecting Dots, when we dip, january 2025

the roster runs through a tight, mostly Tel Aviv-adjacent circle, and the catalogue moves fast for a label not yet two years old, 19 releases on beatport as of this check. a "Dot By Dot" various-artists compilation appeared under a separate DBD catalog prefix, though we could only confirm one volume (DBD002), so we're not calling that an ongoing numbered series yet. the label has also thrown touring showcases under its own name in Mexico City, Cape Town and Tulum. 17 artists we track have released on it, listed below.
