Saved Records

BP DG RA
record label house

Saved Records is Nic Fanciulli's house label out of Maidstone, in the UK, and the founding is told two ways. Nic Fanciulli himself told Miami New Times in 2010 that "Saved started out as a joint venture between myself and Andy Chatterley in 2004." and "We set it up after we’d been working together as Skylark and Buick Project." Mark Fanciulli, talking to Attack Magazine in 2014, put the first record in the same year: "when Saved001 came out in 2004 I thought that both of the tracks were great." Resident Advisor files the label as established in 2005, and Wikipedia says 2005 on Nic Fanciulli's page and 2003 on Andy Chatterley's. so we print 2004 as the label's own count and 2005 as RA's, and leave it at that. Discogs dates SAVED002, Pete Tong's "More Intensity", to january 2005.

today Resident Advisor's line is "Saved Records is a house label run by Nic and Mark Fanciulli." and the Bandcamp bio reads "Label curated and programmed by Nic Fanciulli". Discogs files it as "Record label, based in Maidstone, UK." with Deleted Records as its sub-label. the first names through the door, per Nic Fanciulli: "Our first signings were Joel Mull, Spencer Parker, and Anil Chawla." the catalogue has run long since. SAVED100 is the "Saved 100" compilation from 10 march 2014, SAVED285 is Marco Carola's edit of "Lalo's Groove" from 24 october 2025, Discogs lists 498 releases and Deezer files 281 albums under the label. "We built Saved because no one would sign our records, and it became a home for new artists," Nic Fanciulli told Beatselector in september 2025, when the label was out on its Saved 20 tour with a compilation to go with it. the newest release is Joseph Capriati's "Saudade", 30 october 2026.

there is no radio show or podcast under the label's name. the label's own compilations are the "Collection" albums, Collection C in 2013, Collection D in 2014 and Collection H in 2020, plus "Saved 100" in 2014, and the party side is the Saved nights on Resident Advisor since the mid 2000s, "Nic Fanciulli presents A Decade of Saved" among them. a few things to keep apart from it: Saved Soul Records and the Discogs entry "Saved Records (2)" are other labels, Deleted Records is the sub-label and its releases do not count here, and Nic Fanciulli's Dance or Die parties and his mix CDs are his own, not the label's.

Adam Beyer, Luca Saporito, Marco Carola, Solardo, The Martinez Brothers and Tom Trago are here as remixers, the rest on their own releases or the label's Collection compilations. the spread runs the whole catalogue: Pete Tong's "More Intensity" is SAVED002 from january 2005, Luca Saporito's remix sits on "Guitar 1" (SAVED005, october 2005), The Martinez Brothers remixed "Lost In The Zoo" (SAVED096, 2013), Adam Beyer and Tom Trago both remix on the "Saved 100" compilation, Rampa has a remix on "Collection D" (2014) and "Potty" of his own (2015), Hot Since 82 goes from "Play The Room" (2015) through "Rise" (2020) to a remix of "Witch Doctor" in january 2026, John Summit's "Witch Doctor" itself is from june 2021, Kyle Walker and Max Styler share "Give It Up" (november 2021), and Joseph Capriati's "Saudade" is the newest. 27 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: &ME, Adam Beyer, AMÉMÉ, Andrew Meller, Calussa, Deeper Purpose, Dennis Cruz, Dunmore Brothers, Franky Rizardo, German Brigante, Hot Since 82, Jay de Lys, John Summit, Joseph Capriati, Josh Butler, Kyle Walker, Luca Saporito, Marco Carola, Mason Collective, Max Styler, Pete Tong, Rampa, Rebūke, Sidney Charles, Solardo, The Martinez Brothers, Tom Trago
