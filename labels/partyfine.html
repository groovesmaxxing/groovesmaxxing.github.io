Partyfine

BP DG RA
either lane record label disco · electro · indie · club

Partyfine is Yuksek's label, started in 2013 and run out of his own studio in Reims, France. the label's SoundCloud bio puts it in one line, "A label founded by French electronic music producer and DJ, Yuksek.", and Discogs keeps it to "Electronic music label founded by Yuksek." Resident Advisor lists it as established 2013, and its about text says Yuksek "launches his label Partyfine with a debut EP on May 13th". that EP is the first catalogue number, "Partyfine EP#1" (FINE 001EP), dated 13 May 2013 on Discogs, with Juveniles and Oh Land on it. the base and the working method sit in the same SoundCloud bio: "Independency is a key word for Partyfine. From the will to participate in every production level of an artist - recording, mixing, mastering and distributing, all from under the roof of Yuksek's studio in Reims, France." the name is printed "Partyfine" on Beatport, Resident Advisor, Discogs and in Deezer's label field on 65 albums, and it drifts a little elsewhere: its own Bandcamp storefront prints "PARTY FINE" as two words, Deezer carries "Party Fine" on 7 albums, and the SoundCloud display name is "PARTYFINE". one label, written a few ways. Discogs lists Insane Dances as a sub-label, and its "Studio Partyfine" and "Partyfine Studio" entries are the studio, not the label.

the sound is the founder's taste, in the label's own words. the SoundCloud bio: "Far from any style diktat, one of Partyfine's aims is to present Yuksek's diverse musical tastes, from disco to electro, indie to club." that line is where the genre tag comes from. the same bio gives the reason it exists: "Beyond all, Partyfine was born to gather a family of talented musicians, who can collaborate together around a defined concept using their own backgrounds and desires, releasing great music and throwing down some serious parties." a few markers from the catalogue. VillA "Exotic to Hell" came in 2013, the year SPIN's Philip Sherburne described the label's aesthetic, on 25 November 2013. Yuksek featuring Polo & Pan "Cadenza" landed in 2019, the same year as Partyfine, Vol. 4 "Danse dans le noir", which carries a Purple Disco Machine edit of Yuksek's "Showbiz". that compilation is part of the numbered series "Partyfine, Vol.", six volumes so far: Vol. 1 in 2014, Vol. 4 in January 2019, Vol. 5 "More Or Less Disco" in 2020 and Vol. VI in 2022. we found no Vol. VII, so the series looks paused since 2022. the label itself is not. the newest album is Ubaruh's "Hey, Ubaruh!", 5 June 2026, still on Partyfine, and Electronic Groove called it his debut album. partyfine.com no longer serves the label, so there is no website link here.

a few notes on the names. Purple Disco Machine is here for an edit on the label's own compilation, "Partyfine, Vol. 4 (Danse dans le noir)" (2019), Polo & Pan for "Cadenza" (2019) with Yuksek and for "Recover Vol. 1" (2018), Jean Tonique for "You" (FINE 020CD, 2015), a "Don't Miss" remix (FINE 007EP, 2014) and "Partyfine Vol. 2" (FINE 016LPC, 2015), and Yuksek on his own label from "Partyfine EP#1" (FINE 001EP, 2013), "On My Brain" (2013) and a "Give You Up" remix (FINE 003EP, 2013). 4 artists we track have released on it, a confirmed floor from a catalogue sweep, not the full roster: Jean Tonique, Polo & Pan, Purple Disco Machine, Yuksek

"I started this label with the idea of releasing music from new artists who I can help produce their tracks and give a platform to. Then when I finished my contract with Universal I decide to release my own albums on my label too. So, this label is really about releasing good music when it's ready, no more ambition or career plans."

— Yuksek, founder, When We Dip
