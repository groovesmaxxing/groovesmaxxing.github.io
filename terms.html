short,
in plain words.

groovesmaxxing is a zero-budget project made by a fan. use it, enjoy it, tell us when it's wrong. here's the deal.

last updated 2026-09-24 · tester week version

the deal

it's free. no account, no subscription, nothing to cancel.

every result is a guess, never a confirmation. the percent and the word next to a name say how sure we are. a track named at 60 is a lead, not a fact. check before you buy, book or bet on it.

nothing here is sponsored. no label pays for a place in the library or a spot in a result.

we can change, pause or switch the scan off at any time. it runs on one machine with a monthly cap on catalog lookups. when the cap is hit the scan keeps going with our own library only.

what you agree to

use the scan for what it's for: naming a track you're hearing. keep it to audio you're allowed to record where you are. don't hammer it (it stops you at 30 scans an hour per phone) and don't try to pull audio out of it.

what you send through help us find it? and the correction buttons, you're letting us keep and use to identify tracks and improve the library. no payment, no credit owed. we never publish the clip itself.

what we promise

the library audio never leaves our server. the app never plays, streams or hands out anything from it.

what we keep and why is in the privacy page. it's short too.

what we don't promise

that it works every time on every phone. it's a tester build. it will miss tracks, and now and then it will name the wrong one under a low score.

uptime. if the server is asleep the scan says so and you try again later.

as far as the law allows, we're not liable for what you do with a result, or for the scan being down, wrong or gone.

rights

the site's own words, design and the wordmark are ours. track names, artist names, label names and links belong to whoever made them. they're here because we're pointing at them, not claiming them.

something of yours here that shouldn't be? email us and it comes down.

the boring part

these terms follow the law of colorado, usa. if they change, the date up top changes.
