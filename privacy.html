privacy

what we keep,
and what we never do.

the short version: the scan hears about 15 seconds, tries to name the track, and throws the audio away. an account is optional, email only, no password. no gps, no ads, no analytics. the long version is below, in plain words.

last updated 2026-11-09

the scan

when you tap the button your browser asks for the mic. we get audio only while the scan runs, about 15 seconds, then it stops on its own. nothing is recorded before you tap the button or turn on auto.

auto is for a whole set, and it stays off until you turn it on. while it runs, the phone records a 15 second clip every eight minutes (every twenty once you hit the hourly cap) and keeps the screen awake. auto clips are checked against our own library only. a tap stops it, and after a lock screen or a reload it waits for you to tap again.

the clip goes to our server, which looks for the track in our own library of sets first and in a music recognition catalog second. the catalog is a third party (acrcloud, with audd as a backup when we switch it on). it receives the clip to match it and nothing else about you.

the clip is deleted on the server as soon as the result is in, match or no match. what stays is one line per scan: when it happened, how long it took, what it found and how sure it was. no audio.

after a match, your browser asks spotify, apple music and deezer for the cover and the store links, using the track's name. those requests go straight from your phone, so those services see your address the way any web request does.

"help us find it?"

if the scan comes up empty you can tap help us find it?. that is the only case where we keep a clip. you're sending the same 15 seconds again, plus whatever you type: the venue or event, the dj, the city, a note. we use all of it to hunt the track down.

a kept clip lives until we identify it or for 12 months, whichever comes first, then a job deletes it. want it gone sooner? email us, say roughly when you scanned, and it's gone.

kept clips are fingerprinted, never played, streamed or handed to anyone. the same goes for every recording in our library.

"where are you hearing this?"

under any result you can tell us where you heard it. pick a set and paste its link, and we send the same 15 seconds once more to find the spot in that set, then delete them. the link and any @handle you type stay with that scan. the handle is just a name you gave us, and it only shows up next to a track if we decide to credit you.

your phone

the first time the site needs it, it makes up one random id and keeps it in your browser. that id goes with every call the site makes to our server, opening the scan page included, so we can cap scans per phone (30 an hour), count one answer per phone on each result, and keep your scans and alerts together. it's not tied to your name, number or anything else, and it stays on the phone until you clear it.

your recent scans, the last 50, are listed in your browser. our server also keeps the line for each scan (what the card said and when) under a hash of that id, never the id itself, so your history can follow you if you sign in later. signed out, email us and we delete it. signed in, deleting your account takes it with everything else.

no signal? the clip waits on this phone and goes to our server the moment you're back online. it's deleted from the phone once it's matched, and a clip still waiting after 30 days is dropped.

keep my clips is off until you turn it on under more. then the phone keeps your newest 20 clips for up to 30 days, on this phone only. they never leave it unless you tap scan again or help us find it?. turning it off deletes them.

your crate (the hearts), tonight's auto list, your language and the pages the app saves for offline live in this browser too. clear this phone on more wipes the id, the recent scans and any clips on the phone. it doesn't delete anything on our server; signed in, it also signs this phone out and stops push to it.

no gps, no location. the city you might type into the form is the only place we ever know, and only because you typed it.

alerts

tap tell me when it drops on a scan and we keep that follow with the phone id, or with your account when you're signed in, until the track gets a name or comes out. then we tell you, by push if it's on and by email if you're signed in. follow an artist while signed in and we can also email you when they drop something. each kind of email has its own switch on more.

push stays off until you turn it on and say yes when your browser asks. the browser then gives us an address at its own push service (apple, google, mozilla or microsoft run those), and we keep it with the phone id or your account to send the alert. switch off push on this phone on more, or block it in your browser, and we stop sending.

your account

an account is optional. you sign in with a link or a six digit code sent to your email. no password. the sign-in stays on that phone for up to 180 days, or until you sign out.

an account holds your email, the handle you pick, a badge if you're a verified artist or label, the date you joined, your crate, the artists you follow, the IDs you're waiting on, your scan history, your notification switches, the push address for each phone where push is on, and how many phones you've signed in on. that's what lets your crate and your scans show up on every phone you sign in on.

sign-in links, codes and alert emails go out through our mail provider. turn on fresh grooves friday and we hand your email to buttondown, the newsletter service. turn it off and we ask them to take it off the list.

claim your page and we keep the claim, how you proved it (a code in your soundcloud bio, an instagram message or an email from the address on your page) and any link you showed us. a verified artist or label can invite a team, and the invite goes to the email they type, which we keep with that seat. files sent through the artist tools are kept for fingerprinting, the way the library is, and never played or handed out.

we keep the account until you delete it. on more, download my data gives you your scans, crate, follows and claims as one file. delete my account sends a confirm email, then the crate, follows, alerts, scan history, push addresses and sign-ins go, and your email and handle are wiped. your claims stay on record, without them. answers from the correction buttons and set spots stay too, as an anonymous phone's word.

corrections

that's right and it's actually... send your answer with the phone id. we keep those to fix the library. nothing changes on one vote alone.

what we don't do

no passwords. no ads, no analytics scripts, no trackers of our own. we don't sell or share anything about you. there's nothing to sell.

players, covers and links

the fonts come from this site.

the home page and the artist, label and tracklist pages embed players from spotify, soundcloud and youtube, and a few carry instagram or beatport ones. most load with the page, before you press play, so those services see the visit and can set their own cookies under their own privacy rules. our youtube players use youtube's no-cookie address, and many start as a thumbnail that loads the player only when you tap it. tracklist pages also load youtube's or soundcloud's player script so the list can follow the set, and radio loads youtube's after your first tap. the beatport samples on dropping soon load only when you tap play.

set thumbnails come from youtube's image servers. newsletter pages show covers from spotify, soundcloud, youtube and buttondown, and the cover on a scan result comes from spotify, apple music or deezer. the newsletter forms on the site (home, newsletter, more) send your email to buttondown.

links to stores and platforms are plain links. tap one and you're on their site, under their rules.

the server

the site is hosted on cloudflare pages, so cloudflare sees the request data every host sees (your address, your browser). the scan and the accounts run on our own machine in the united states, reached through tailscale's network.

the server writes one line per request: the route, the status, the milliseconds, and a short hash of the phone id (or of your address when there's no id), never the id itself.

kids

the scan and the accounts aren't built for anyone under 13 and we don't knowingly keep anything from them. if that happened, email us and it's deleted.

you

want something deleted, or want to know what we hold? signed in, download my data and delete my account are on more. signed out, or for anything else, email us. a person answers, not a form.

a lawyer reads this before it's final. when anything changes, the date up top changes.
