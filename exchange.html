how the price works

each track gets one closing price a day, set by where it ranks against every other track with dj support that day. three things push it:

  • dj support: where it ranks for dj support, plus where it ranks for support gained this week.
  • charts: its best beatport chart tier in the last two weeks. a higher tier, a bigger lift.
  • editorial: spotify editorial playlist adds in the last four weeks.

the method in plain words, version v2:

each day every listed track is ranked against the rest of the market. 55% where it ranks for dj support, 15% where it ranks for support gained this week, 20% its best beatport chart tier in the last two weeks, 10% spotify editorial adds in the last four weeks. smoothed over a few days and scaled to 5-250 points.

every price starts at a floor of 5. indices are the average price of their seasoned members, set to 1000 ninety days ago. a track with no close to compare yet shows as new, not as zero. g-mo's book scores each pick from the close on the day it was picked, and a pick too new to score stays on the list, marked.

points show where a track stands against the rest of the market, not a count. we only show our own points, percent moves and rounded tiers, never raw counts. these are v2 weights and they can change.
