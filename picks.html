how the scoreboard is read: a pick is a record we added to good vibe juice or bumpy for fresh grooves friday that month, plus five we name by hand. on the day we pick it we note its dj support tier (how many different djs played it in published sets, counted by songstats) and its editorial playlist adds. when the month ends we read it again. a hit moved up a dj support tier, entered a beatport top 20 or got a new spotify editorial add. a move gained 5 or more djs or 2 or more editorial adds. flat means neither, with the record out two weeks or more. too soon means it came out under two weeks before the month ended, so it carries into the next read. dates read month.day. tiers and counts only, never dj names or set lists. a dash means songstats has no data for that record yet.
