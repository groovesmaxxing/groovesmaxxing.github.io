Mau P

🚧 bumpy amsterdam tech house

Maurits Westveen spent years making big-room edm under a different name. then the world shut down, he rebuilt from scratch, and came back as Mau P with a record about drugs from amsterdam that went straight to number one on beatport.

the students now became the teacher.

on the switch, 2022

both parents were musicians. his father a saxophonist, conductor and arranger, his mother a singer. his dad died when he was eighteen. he still makes records in his father's studio.

what followed: "Metro" with Kevin de Vries on Afterlife, the most-played track at tomorrowland in 2023. a residency at the Wynn in vegas. baddest behaviour, his own show, past 25,000 people across sold-out 2024 dates and now a second straight run of wednesdays at Pacha Ibiza. dj mag top 100 at 91 in 2024, then 77 in 2025.

for 2026 he has promised an album, a label and a clothing line. none of it is out yet.

Michael Bibi

🚧 bumpy london tech house

london, wimbledon specifically. his father is Robin Bibi, a blues guitarist who has played with Robert Plant and Ben E. King, so the music was in the house long before it was a job.

he co-founded Solid Grooves Records with PAWSA in 2015, though the party had been running a couple of years by then. "Hanging Tree" on Repopulate Mars in 2018 is the one that broke him wide. tech house at the dj awards in ibiza the year after. then SG Raw, then ISOLAT, then ISOLATE, his own all-night concept that came out of a meditation retreat in jamaica.

in may 2023 he was diagnosed with CNS lymphoma. he told everyone himself, the same week.

I don't know what lies ahead, I'm tired but I know I am strong & I won't let this beat me.

5 june 2023

four months later he walked into DC-10 for the solid grooves closing party and played a surprise set, days after finishing his main treatment.

When I first came into hospital I was barely able to walk. Today I walk out with a smile having completed my main treatment.

28 september 2023

he announced remission that december. in april 2024 he played coachella at sunset. that july he filled a 45,000-capacity show at finsbury park and called it One Life, for the city that raised him and treated him, then gave £124,832 of it to the royal marsden cancer charity. he did it again in 2025 and gave another £113,943. at his two-year scans in november 2025 he was still in remission.

Today was a good day. Thank you everyone that helped get us here and danced along the way.

14 november 2025

his line for all of it is one life, live it. that one is his, not ours.

london, united kingdom

runs

PAWSA

🚧 bumpy london tech house

David Esekhile, london. his father is nigerian, his mother is irish, and they met at a cinema in the city he still works out of. he sent a demo to Leftwing : Kody, who signed it on the spot, and the four-track "Pilot" ep landed on Lost Records in february 2014.

You can create an imaginary world if you become an artist: that's why I chose to make music as PAWSA, not David Esekhile.

dj mag, undated

a year later he co-founded Solid Grooves Records with Michael Bibi, two names up this page. dj mag gave it best label at the best of british awards in 2023. PAWZ came in 2017 for the records that did not fit, and he co-runs GOLFOS TRAXX with Dennis Cruz.

2024 is when the numbers went strange. "PICK UP THE PHONE" with Nate Dogg, then "TOO COOL TO BE CARELESS", both on his own PAWZ. his rework of "Dirty Cash (Money Talks)" now sits past 257 million plays on spotify.

For me, a label is about curation and creating a home for work that fits a certain standard and a certain identity. A label should feel like a language, not just a release schedule.

nylon, 9 april 2026

august 2025 he took over gunnersbury park for a full day and called it pawsa in the park, his biggest uk show at the time. he entered the dj mag top 100 at 69 that october, his first time on it. february 2026 he headlined alexandra palace. he took pawsa in the park to amsterdam in july.

Solomun

either lane hamburg melodic house + techno

Mladen Solomun was born in travnik in 1975, in what was then yugoslavia, and grew up in hamburg after his family moved. germany offered him a place on the under-16 football team. he turned it down to play for the country he was born in.

the record that took him worldwide was a remix. Noir & Haze, "Around", on Noir Music in 2011. fifteen years later it is still his second most-played track on spotify, past 66 million.

he co-founded Diynamic with Adriano Trolio in hamburg, and the label turns twenty in 2026. then 2DIY4 in 2010 for the stranger material, NINL for his 2021 album, and Love Recycled in june 2025, built entirely around reworking old records.

I know from personal experience that 60-90 minutes is not enough to really take the audience on a journey

rolling stone uk, 3 october 2024

which is the whole point of him. Solomun +1 at Pacha Ibiza runs nineteen sundays in 2026, his twelfth straight year, and he plays five of them alone all night. march brought "Kinesphere" on Diynamic. july brought "Rumpta", his first record with Skrillex.

in june he opened Pacha New York for free, and gave the tickets to people who had come to his fish market show in the bronx.

I held that rule for over ten years. I never questioned it. Once the summer starts, I stay in Europe.

on breaking it, june 2026

he sat out both coachella weekends this april after surgery on his left ear, and told everyone himself rather than let it be a rumour.

The doctor says everything went well, so in the near future I'll most likely hear just as well or just as badly as before.

19 march 2026

Vintage Culture

either lane são paulo house

Lukas Ruiz, born in mundo novo, out on the border between brazil and paraguay. he was studying law in maringá. he taught himself to produce instead.

The internet was my school.

dj mag, 15 march 2022

an unofficial rework of pink floyd's "Another Brick In The Wall" went up on soundcloud around 2014 and did close to twenty million views on youtube. six years later "It Is What It Is" with Elise LeGrow on Defected gave him his first number one on the overall beatport chart. his remix of Maverick Sabre and Jorja Smith's "Slow Down" is still his biggest, past 395 million spotify plays.

Promised Land, his debut album, arrived in may 2024 after four years of work. in 2025 he started AFFAIRS, which is a label and a party and a lifestyle line at the same time.

june 2026 he took Affairs to Pacha Ibiza for four tuesdays and picked every lineup himself. Eric Prydz on the first, WhoMadeWho on the second, Franky Rizardo back to back on the third, and a brazilian closer with Mochakk, Volkoder and Doozie. in april he put out "Freaky 1" with Max Styler and Ali Love, two entries down this page.

It's an absolute honor to finally release a track we wrote with Ali Love in Amsterdam three years ago.

beatportal, 10 april 2026

he keeps a home in são paulo, where his mother lives with him, and spends the summer on ibiza. he finished 2025 at eleven on the dj mag hundred.

Max Styler

🚧 bumpy california indie dance + tech house

san luis obispo, california. he opened for Above & Beyond while he was still in high school, which turned into a support slot for Steve Aoki, which turned into a deal. "Badman" with Autoerotique came out on Dim Mak in november 2014 and aoki called it one of his biggest club records of the year.

that version of him made trap and electro. the one you know now started in january 2023 with the "Wanna Dance" ep on John Summit's Experts Only. a fan later turned the title into a bracelet and handed it to him, which he says is still the best thing anyone has given him.

he closed 2025 as beatport's number one top-selling artist in indie dance and number two in tech house, and they made him artist of the month that january.

I almost think that the whole 'one big song' thing is a little bit dated now.

beatportal, 6 january 2026

in october 2025 he started Nu Moda and moved every 2026 release onto it.

Nu Moda basically means 'new style.' It's a reminder to push the boundaries, not do the same thing twice, and look for other people who are naturally in that same mindset.

april took him to the yuma tent at coachella and put "Freaky 1" out with Vintage Culture and Ali Love. he is on the 2026 guest list for Solomun +1, and he takes lollapalooza chicago on 1 august.

TOBEHONEST

🚧 bumpy los angeles tech house

Luke learned to dj at fraternity parties at the university of arizona, then moved to los angeles for a steady job and lost it to music. asked once what he listens to, he named Max Styler and Solomun, both further up this page.

Groovy, nostalgic, and impactful.

his sound in three words, january 2025

two records got him on the board in 2022, "Daka" on Bump N' Grind and "Maquina De Bailar" on Black Book, both beatport top 100. "Horny" with San Pacho on Insomniac in february 2025 is his most streamed.

but the one he points at himself is "Evil Ways", out on Helix in june 2025 and built on a cleared Willie Bobo sample.

Every time I play it, people sing to me, and it's slowly becoming everybody's favorite song.

AYYBO

🚧 bumpy anaheim tech house

Aaron Bonnema, anaheim, california. he came up through high school drumline, released as Bonnema until 2022, and had already been signed to Zeds Dead's Deadbeats back in 2018 under that name.

"HYPNOSIS" with ero808 landed on FUNKSHWAY in july 2022 and changed the shape of things. Pitbull remixed it the following year. he writes his own lyrics and sings on his own records, which almost nobody in this lane does.

John Summit

🚧 bumpy chicago tech house

John Schuster, from naperville just outside chicago. he studied accounting at illinois, passed his cpa, and spent his days at Ernst & Young until he walked out in 2019 to make records. the stage name is a ski thing.

I was just like, 'I need a DJ name, I love skiing in the outdoors, I'm gonna name myself Summit.'

dancing astronaut, 16 september 2023

he put "Deep End" out himself in 2020 after a clip took off, then Defected picked it up in june and it went to number one on beatport overall. the year after, he was the top-selling artist on beatport across every genre.

From there I got reached out to from about every major label to sign it, but since Defected has always been the goal for me, I knew that would be a perfect home for the track.

beatportal, 7 july 2020

his label started in may 2022 as Off The Grid and became Experts Only in september 2023 after a dispute over the name. same ski sign the stage name came from. it topped beatport's global label chart in 2024 and most of 2025, and it is where Max Styler's "Wanna Dance" ep and AYYBO's "Obsession" came out, both further up this page.

CTRL ESCAPE, his second album, landed in april 2026. he built Experts Only Festival on randall's island in new york, over fifty thousand people at the first one, and the second runs this september. june through july he held down nine straight mondays at [UNVRS], his first ibiza residency. on 7 and 8 august he is in aspen co-headlining with Dom Dolla, one entry down.

Dom Dolla

🚧 bumpy melbourne house + tech house

Dominic Matheson was born in manila to australian parents, raised in darwin and then melbourne, and cut his teeth in melbourne clubs. "Take It" on Sweat It Out in 2018 is what put him on the map in america. "San Frandisco" the year after won him his first aria.

he has now taken best dance release at the arias four times, and 2023, 2024 and 2025 back to back made him the first artist to win it three years running. in november 2025 they gave him the first ever aria global impact award. he picked up a grammy nomination in 2024 for his remix of Gorillaz, "New Gold."

the collaborator list reads like a dare. MK, Tove Lo, Sonny Fodera, Kid Cudi, Daya, and Nelly Furtado on "Eat Your Man", which went platinum in australia and canada.

last december he put 40,000 people in allianz stadium in sydney and became the first electronic act to headline an australian stadium. this september he does marvel stadium in melbourne, which sold out almost immediately.

Melbourne clubs are where I cut my teeth as a DJ. I don't get to play at home as often as I'd like these days, so after touring all over the world and learning what makes a great show, I wanted this one to be incredibly special.

on the marvel stadium show, 2 june 2026

RÜFÜS DU SOL

🧃 vibes sydney melodic house, played live

three of them, out of sydney since november 2010. Tyrone Lindqvist on vocals and guitar, Jon George on keys and synths, James Hunt on drums, though they swap in the studio. they were just RÜFÜS at home. a trademark problem in america made them RÜFÜS DU SOL there in 2014, and they took the longer name worldwide in 2018.

Atlas went straight to number one in australia in 2013. Bloom in 2016, out on ODESZA's Foreign Family Collective, is the one that travelled. they started Rose Avenue Records in 2018, named after the house near venice beach where they lived and made Solace.

they won the grammy for best dance recording in 2022 with "Alive", and have been nominated five more times, most recently this year for Inhale / Exhale.

Our show is a spectacle. It's a live experience and there's humanity in it. There's human error. From night to night, the performance might be slightly different.

james hunt, los angeles times, 15 august 2025

last august they filled the rose bowl with around sixty thousand people, the first electronic act to play it. in december they were handed a plaque for the highest-selling electronic tour ever recorded. this year they did four nights at madison square garden, and five at the kia forum land across august, every one of them still to come.

When we write music, we aren't necessarily thinking about a vocalist or specific aesthetic. We're searching for a feeling.

tyrone lindqvist, musictech, 5 november 2021

Jamie xx

either lane london house + uk garage

on his own he started with "Far Nearer" in 2011, then In Colour in 2015, which took a mercury nomination, a grammy nomination and number three in the uk. In Waves followed in 2024.

Solomun, further up this page, sent him two unsolicited remixes of "Waited All Night" and both came out in august 2025. he guested at Solomun +1 in ibiza on 12 july.

Polo & Pan

SOFI TUKKER

🧃 vibes brooklyn house + brazilian pop

Sophie Hawley-Weld and Tucker Halpern met at brown in 2014, at a gallery where she was performing. he had captained the basketball team until illness ended that. they live in the same williamsburg apartment with a third friend and have never been a couple, which people ask about constantly.

she fell for bossa nova as a teenager and learned portuguese so she could sing it properly, which is why half their catalogue is in it. "Drinkee" took its words from "Relógio", a poem by the brazilian writer Chacal, and got them a grammy nomination for best dance recording in 2017. Treehouse took another in 2019.

they started Animal Talk in 2018 as a label and a collective, and relaunched it in 2025 as a management company.

butter in may 2025 was the one they had been promising themselves, recorded in brazil with Seu Jorge, Liniker, Silva and Rael on it.
