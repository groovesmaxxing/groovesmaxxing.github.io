how it works

tap once near the speaker. we listen for up to 15 seconds and start matching at four, then every couple of seconds. a sure match stops the scan early.

we check our own library of sets first, then the big catalogs.

can't name it? you choose whether we keep the clip to keep hunting. nothing is kept unless you say so.

your scans stay on this phone, and every result has a button to tell us we got it wrong.

hearing a set? under the result, tell us which one. we send the clip once more to find the spot in it, then delete it.

at a set, turn on auto. the phone scans on its own every eight minutes, keeps the screen awake, and builds tonight's list as it goes. tap the rings or the auto button to stop.

no signal? the clip stays on this phone and gets matched the moment you are back online.
