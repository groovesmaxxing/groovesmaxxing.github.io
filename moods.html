what's the mood?

pick one or type your own. you get 30 tracks from our four playlists, every one out now and one tap from spotify.

every track comes from one of our four playlists: good vibe juice, bumpy, waaavy and wen playa. we only add what survives a week of listening, so nothing here is filler, and nothing is unreleased.

each mood is a rule over those playlists, plus the tempo, key and genre where we know them. type a feeling, a place, a genre, a tempo like 122 bpm, or an artist we play. it all runs in your browser, nothing you type is sent anywhere.

want them in spotify? copy the links, open a playlist in the spotify desktop app and paste.
